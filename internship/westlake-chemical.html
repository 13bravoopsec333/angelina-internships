📝 Description

Internships in engineering (mechanical, chemical, electrical), operations, chemistry, and business. Hands-on experience in petrochemical manufacturing. Mentored by professionals.

✅ Eligibility

Completed 2nd year of college, GPA 3.0+, authorized to work in US

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Westlake's careers page (westlake.com/careers). Westlake is a petrochemical and building products company headquartered in Houston, TX. Search for 'intern' or 'co-op' positions. Relevant roles include: Process Engineering Intern, Quality Engineering Intern, Materials/Chemical Engineering Intern. Westlake also recruits at university career fairs — check if they attend UF career events.

📄 Resume Tips

Westlake produces PVC, polyethylene, and other polymers — highlight polymer chemistry coursework and Sumerlin group research. NMR and FTIR for polymer characterization are directly relevant. JMP for statistical process control. Creo/Solidworks for equipment design. PPAP and GD&T from HNI show manufacturing quality awareness. Emphasize any chemical engineering or process engineering coursework alongside materials.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Westlake's primary locations are in Texas (Calvert City, KY and Lake Charles, LA also). Be open to relocation. The company values hands-on manufacturing experience — your HNI internship with GD&T and PPAP is a strong asset. Apply early — Houston-area chemical companies recruit in fall. Network with Westlake engineers through AIChE or MSE professional organizations. Westlake has been growing through acquisitions — check for new facility openings.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with recruiter (20-30 min), followed by technical video interview with engineering team (45-60 min). Focus on polymer processing knowledge, analytical skills, and manufacturing quality. May include a plant tour or on-site interview for final candidates. Behavioral questions about working in industrial/manufacturing settings.

🏆 Success Stories

Westlake interns gain experience in petrochemical production and polymer processing. The company's building products division (Vinyl) provides exposure to polymer compounding and extrusion. Many interns convert to full-time process engineering roles at Westlake facilities.
