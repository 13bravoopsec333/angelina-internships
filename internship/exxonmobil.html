📝 Description

Internships in engineering, geoscience, research, and business. Exposure to energy industry operations and projects.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled students, various STEM and business majors

📋 Application Process

Apply through jobs.exxonmobil.com. Complete detailed online application with resume, transcript, GPA, and education history. ExxonMobil uses a structured recruiting process with campus-specific timelines. Initial screening by HR, followed by a multi-round interview process: phone screen with recruiter, technical interview with a hiring manager, and a final round that may include a panel interview or site visit. The process can take 4-8 weeks. Background check and drug screening required.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through ExxonMobil's careers portal and through your university's career services. ExxonMobil recruits heavily at top engineering schools and targets specific universities for campus recruiting. Attend their information sessions and career fair booths — they often extend interview invitations on the spot or shortly after events. Networking with ExxonMobil engineers through alumni connections or professional societies is very effective. Apply early — positions close quickly due to high volume.

📄 Resume Tips

For petroleum/chemicals, emphasize coursework in thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, process engineering, and materials science. Your materials engineering background is relevant for materials selection, corrosion engineering, and process optimization. Highlight research experience (NMR, TLC, polymer chemistry) — analytical skills are highly valued. Mention JMP for statistical analysis. If you have any process engineering or manufacturing experience from HNI, emphasize it. One page, extremely professional formatting. ExxonMobil values precision and attention to detail in every application aspect.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

ExxonMobil is one of the most competitive internship programs in engineering. They value strong academics (GPA 3.5+ preferred, 3.7+ for R&D), demonstrated leadership, and genuine interest in the energy industry. Your materials engineering background is relevant for materials engineering and R&D roles. Your Sumerlin research experience demonstrates analytical rigor that ExxonMobil values. Apply to multiple locations — Houston HQ has the most diverse opportunities. Demonstrate knowledge of ExxonMobil's business (upstream, downstream, chemicals, low carbon solutions) and how your skills apply. Be prepared for a rigorous technical interview covering engineering fundamentals.

🎤 Interview Format

Multi-round process: phone screen with recruiter (30 min, behavioral + GPA + career interests), followed by a technical interview with the hiring manager (45-60 min, deep technical questions on engineering fundamentals and your research), and a final round that may be a panel interview with 3-4 engineers or a virtual site visit. Questions are rigorous and cover both technical depth and breadth. Behavioral questions focus on leadership, teamwork, and handling challenging situations. Expect questions about your specific research and how it applies to ExxonMobil's business.

🏆 Success Stories

ExxonMobil hires from top engineering programs but also values students from state schools with exceptional GPAs and relevant experience. Materials engineering students with strong research backgrounds are competitive for R&D and materials engineering roles. The internship is a pipeline to full-time employment — ExxonMobil invests heavily in intern development and many interns receive return offers. Students who demonstrate both technical excellence and business awareness tend to succeed.

📝 Supplemental Questions

ExxonMobil often includes supplemental questions about career interests, specific technical areas, and why you want to work in the energy industry. Some roles ask about willingness to relocate or work in specific locations. Research ExxonMobil's business segments and low carbon solutions strategy — showing awareness of their energy transition efforts differentiates you. Be specific about which career path (engineering, R&D, operations) interests you and why.
