📝 Description

Co-op and internship opportunities across engineering and business. Terms: Spring (Jan-Apr), Summer (May-Aug), Fall (Aug-Dec). Materials Science co-ops available.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled in engineering or business program at accredited university. Spring, Summer, Fall terms available.

📋 Application Process

Apply through Honda careers portal (careers.honda.com). Their process is well-documented on the website: Step 1: Find jobs → Step 2: Complete profile & apply → Step 3: Pre-screening call with Recruiter → Step 4: Interview with hiring team → Step 5: Offer & pre-employment screening → Step 6: Welcome to team Honda. Standard application: resume + cover letter. Honda's co-op program allows alternating semesters of work and school. Multiple locations: Russells Point, OH (Honda R&D/manufacturing) and Lincoln, AL (manufacturing).

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through careers.honda.com. Check Handshake — Honda actively recruits through Handshake. Honda recruits at major engineering universities. For Russells Point, OH location, Ohio State is a primary target school. For Lincoln, AL, recruit from southeast universities including UF. Attend Honda information sessions at career fairs. LinkedIn networking with Honda engineers and recruiters. Honda's co-op program is a significant pipeline — apply early and express interest in alternating co-op semesters.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, GPA 3.96, Creo/Solidworks CAD (Honda uses CATIA primarily but values CAD proficiency), GD&T (automotive manufacturing standard), Ansys Granta (materials selection for vehicle components), PPAP experience (automotive quality standard — CRITICAL for Honda). Emphasize: manufacturing processes, quality systems, lean manufacturing knowledge. HNI internship experience is very relevant — Honda and HNI both value manufacturing excellence. Mention Gator Blacksmithing (hands-on fabrication), Material Advantage (materials professional society). Honda values 'mutual respect and mindfulness' — demonstrate collaborative mindset.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) Honda's co-op program is structured — apply for specific co-op rotations, not just summer internships. 2) Russells Point, OH is Honda's main US R&D center — highly desirable. 3) Honda values quality and continuous improvement — mention PPAP and HNI manufacturing experience prominently. 4) Research Honda's current initiatives: EV transition, hydrogen fuel cells, autonomous driving. 5) Honda's culture emphasizes respect and collaboration — demonstrate these values in your application. 6) Apply through multiple channels (careers.honda.com + Handshake + career fairs). 7) Express willingness to do multiple co-op rotations — Honda values commitment. 8) The pre-screening call with Recruiter is important — prepare for behavioral questions.

🎤 Interview Format

Pre-screening call with Recruiter (15-20 min, behavioral), then interview with hiring team (1-2 rounds, mix of behavioral and technical). Technical questions about manufacturing processes, materials properties, and quality systems. Behavioral questions about teamwork, problem-solving, and Honda's values. May include a tour of the facility. The hiring team interview is often panel-style with the manager and team members.

🏆 Success Stories

Honda's co-op/internship program is well-regarded on Reddit (r/MechanicalEngineering, r/engineering). Interns describe a structured program with real manufacturing exposure. Successful candidates typically have strong GPA, manufacturing interest, and quality systems knowledge. Honda values 'Team Honda' culture — demonstrating collaboration is key. Former co-ops note that the rotating co-op structure provides excellent career development. Reddit users emphasize that Honda values practical manufacturing skills over research experience. The pre-screening call is described as a casual behavioral assessment.

📝 Supplemental Questions

None — standard resume + cover letter application. However, the online profile requires detailed information about education, skills, and experience.
