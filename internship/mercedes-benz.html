📝 Description

Summer internship program. Engineering roles at MBUSI (Tuscaloosa) and corporate roles in Atlanta. Quality audit, ML/AI, powertrain engineering.

✅ Eligibility

Rising college junior or senior. Motivated students seeking real-world experience.

📋 Application Process

Apply through Mercedes-Benz careers portal (jobs.mercedes-benz.com). The MB STAR program is Mercedes-Benz's flagship internship program for students. Application requires: resume, cover letter, transcript, and supplemental short-answer questions (typically about career goals, why Mercedes-Benz, leadership experience). Multi-stage process: online application, online assessment, phone/video interview, final interview with hiring manager. Competitive program with structured timeline.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through jobs.mercedes-benz.com for MB STAR positions. Check Handshake for Mercedes-Benz postings. Mercedes-Benz USA HQ is in Atlanta, GA — they recruit heavily at southeast universities including UF. Attend Mercedes-Benz information sessions at UF career fairs. LinkedIn networking with Mercedes-Benz recruiters and engineers. The MB STAR program has specific application windows — monitor their careers page starting in fall.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, GPA 3.96, Creo/Solidworks CAD (automotive design), GD&T (critical for automotive manufacturing), Ansys Granta (materials selection for vehicle components), PPAP experience (automotive quality standard — VERY relevant), manufacturing experience from HNI internship. Emphasize: automotive materials knowledge, manufacturing processes, quality systems, supply chain awareness. Mention Gator Blacksmithing (metalworking skills + leadership as Secretary), Material Advantage. Any experience with metals, polymers, composites, or coatings is relevant. JMP statistical analysis for quality control.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) Mercedes-Benz values diversity — mention any unique perspectives or leadership roles. 2) The MB STAR program is competitive — tailor your application specifically to Mercedes-Benz (not generic). 3) Research Mercedes-Benz's current initiatives: EV transition, lightweight materials, sustainability. 4) Mention any interest in automotive industry specifically. 5) Atlanta, GA is the USA HQ — apply to that location first. 6) Mercedes-Benz values teamwork and collaboration — emphasize group project experience. 7) The supplemental questions are important — answer thoughtfully and specifically. 8) Apply early — MB STAR has fixed application windows.

🎤 Interview Format

Multi-stage: online assessment (personality/logic), phone screening with recruiter, video interview with hiring manager, and final panel interview. Behavioral questions dominate: STAR method responses about leadership, teamwork, problem-solving. Technical questions about engineering fundamentals and automotive materials. May include a case study or presentation. The final interview is often with the team you'd be working with.

🏆 Success Stories

MB STAR intern alumni frequently post on LinkedIn about their experience. The program is described as structured with mentorship, project ownership, and exposure to senior leadership. Successful candidates typically have strong GPA, relevant engineering coursework, and demonstrated interest in automotive/mobility. Reddit users on r/MechanicalEngineering note that Mercedes-Benz values teamwork, innovation, and cultural fit. Former interns highlight the global exposure and networking opportunities. The program has a high conversion rate to full-time offers.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Typically 2-3 short-answer questions: Why Mercedes-Benz? Describe a time you showed leadership. What are your career goals? How does this internship fit your development? Each response should be 150-300 words. Tailor responses to Mercedes-Benz's values: passion, performance, integrity.
