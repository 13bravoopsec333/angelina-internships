📝 Description

10-week summer research internship at one of the nation's leading multidisciplinary S&T labs. Students work with world-renowned scientists on materials science, electronics, optics, and more.

✅ Eligibility

US citizens, enrolled undergrad/grad students

📋 Application Process

1) Apply through NREIP (Naval Research Enterprise Internship Program) portal at asee.org/nreip 2) Submit transcript, resume, personal statement, research interest statement, and 1-2 recommendation letters 3) Application reviewed by program office 4) Matched with NRL lab/mentor based on research interests 5) Notification in ~8-12 weeks after deadline. Deadlines are typically in fall (October-November) for summer positions. The DoN-NRL program is specifically for Naval Research Laboratory. Must be US citizen. Competitive — ~20-30% acceptance rate.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through the NREIP portal (asee.org/nreip) — this is the official channel for DoN-NRL internships. Also check NRL's website directly for other internship opportunities. Express interest in specific NRL research divisions (Materials Science, Chemistry, Physics, Engineering). Get strong recommendation letters from Sumerlin group faculty. Write a compelling personal statement connecting your research experience to NRL's mission. Apply early — deadlines are typically in fall.

🎤 Interview Format

NREIP typically does not have a formal interview process. Selection is based on application materials: GPA, research experience, recommendation letters, personal statement, and research interest alignment with available mentors. Some labs may conduct a brief phone screen or video call before final selection, but most decisions are made from the application alone. If interviewed, expect questions about your research experience, analytical techniques, and career goals.

🏆 Success Stories
