📝 Description

10-12 week paid internships across engineering, software development, design, and business. Hands-on projects in critical vehicle technology, battery storage research, supply chain transformation.

✅ Eligibility

Rising junior/senior, pursuing BS/MS/PhD in ME, EE, Manufacturing Engineering, etc. Min GPA 3.0 (3.4 preferred). Must be authorized to work in US without sponsorship.

📋 Application Process

Apply through apply.ford.com or careers.ford.com/students-graduates → Select internship posting → Complete application (may include Workday profile) → Recruiter screening → Virtual interviews (2-3 rounds) → Offer. Ford Summer Intern Program is 10-12 weeks, paid. Applications typically open in fall for summer positions.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Directly through Ford's careers portal (apply.ford.com). Join Ford's Talent Network for alerts. Attend career fairs where Ford recruits (they visit many top engineering schools). Also check LinkedIn for specific Ford internship postings.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight engineering coursework, CAD skills (Creo/SolidWorks from HNI), and manufacturing experience. Ford values: curiosity, courage, and collaboration. Mention any automotive interest or experience. Emphasize process improvement, GD&T, and quality systems. GPA 3.5+ preferred.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Ford's summer intern program is highly competitive — apply early and tailor your application to specific roles (manufacturing, quality, supply chain, software). Express genuine interest in automotive/mobility industry. Research Ford's EV and sustainability initiatives. Connect with Ford recruiters and current interns on LinkedIn.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone/video screen → 1-2 virtual panel interviews. Mix of behavioral (STAR format) and technical questions. Ford uses structured behavioral interviewing. Expect questions about teamwork, leadership, and innovation.

🏆 Success Stories

Ford's internship program is one of the most well-known in automotive. Interns work on real projects with significant visibility. Many interns convert to full-time through Ford College Graduate (FCG) program. Cross-functional learning opportunities are a major draw.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Expect: Why Ford? Describe a project where you demonstrated leadership. How do you approach cross-functional collaboration? What excites you about the future of mobility?
