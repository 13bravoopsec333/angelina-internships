📝 Description

Chemical Engineering co-ops and internships in Product Development, R&D, and operations. 4-month co-op terms (spring/fall) and summer internships. Rotational experience across divisions.

✅ Eligibility

GPA 3.0+, completed sophomore year, enrolled at accredited university

📋 Application Process

Apply online via careers.olin.com → Application review → Phone screen → Virtual/on-site interview → Offer. Posting windows open seasonally (spring for summer, fall for spring co-ops). Rolling reviews.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Directly through olin.com/careers/us-college-recruiting/ portal. Also recruit at career fairs at target schools (SEC, ACC engineering programs). LinkedIn application is secondary.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight chemical engineering coursework and any hands-on lab/manufacturing experience. Olin values safety mindset and process improvement. Quantify impact in any prior roles. Mention GPA if above 3.5. Include keywords: process engineering, chemical processes, safety, Six Sigma.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Attend Olin's campus recruiting events if they visit UF. Tailor resume to the specific division (Epoxy, Chlor Alkali, Winchester/Ammunition). Connect with Olin recruiters on LinkedIn. Apply early when postings open. Olin is big on manufacturing excellence — emphasize any production/facility experience from HNI.

🎤 Interview Format

1-2 rounds: initial phone/video screen with recruiter, then virtual panel interview with hiring manager and team members. Behavioral + technical questions. Expect questions about process safety, teamwork, and why Olin specifically.

🏆 Success Stories

Olin has grown many college recruits into plant leaders and supply chain directors. Their internship-to-full-time conversion rate is strong for engineering roles. Past interns report meaningful project ownership and exposure to senior leadership.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Likely includes: Why Olin? Describe a time you worked on a team to solve a problem. What interests you about the chemical/ammo industry? Safety-related scenario questions.
