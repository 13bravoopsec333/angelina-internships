Wood products manufacturing internship. Mechanical, electrical, manufacturing, and industrial engineering roles at manufacturing sites.

✅ Eligibility

At least 18, minimum Sophomore standing, pursuing BS in Mechanical, Electrical, Manufacturing, or Industrial Engineering

📋 Application Process

1) Create profile on Weyerhaeuser Taleo careers portal. 2) Apply to specific internship posting. 3) Recruiter resume review. 4) Phone screening. 5) Virtual or on-site interview with hiring manager. 6) Offer. Average time-to-hire: 31 days per Glassdoor. Internships focus on forestry, lumber manufacturing, supply chain, and wood products operations.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly through weyerhaeuser.taleo.net and filter for 'Engineering, Science, & Architecture' roles. Attend SFI Annual Conference if possible. Join Weyerhaeuser Talent Community at weyerhaeuser.com/careers/weyerhaeuser-opportunities1/ to get updates. Locations include Seattle WA HQ and lumber mills across the US and Canada.

📄 Resume Tips

Emphasize materials science coursework — wood products are materials engineering. Highlight any manufacturing or process engineering experience. Your Creo/SolidWorks skills are relevant for product design roles. Mention Ansys Granta for materials selection knowledge. If you have any forestry, sustainability, or green building coursework, highlight it. Your polymer research group experience shows lab/research capability.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Join the Weyerhaeuser Talent Community for early notification of openings. Apply to roles in both Seattle WA and manufacturing locations (lumber mills) — less competition outside HQ. Express interest in their sustainability and forest products innovation. Your materials engineering background directly applies to wood science and engineered wood products (LVL, glulam, I-joists). Network at forestry/sustainability conferences.

Difficulty rated 2.8/5 on Glassdoor. 75% positive interview experience. Questions about interest in wood products industry and career goals.
