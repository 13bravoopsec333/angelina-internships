📝 Description

12-week student vacation program. Mining, energy, and infrastructure projects. Real projects and practical industry skills.

✅ Eligibility

Working towards Bachelor/Master's degree in engineering. Strong academic record. 12-week program.

📋 Application Process

Applications open on hatch.com/careers (Workday ATS), typically in August–October for the following summer. Hatch calls their internship the 'Student Vacation Program' — it's a 12–16 week summer placement. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major match). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Technical/behavioral interview with hiring manager (45–60 min). (5) Offer within 2–4 weeks. Hatch operates across metals & minerals, energy, infrastructure, and digital sectors. Major US offices include Pittsburgh, PA and Chicago, IL.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on hatch.com/careers. Also available through Handshake and university career fairs. Hatch recruits heavily at mining, materials, and metals engineering programs. UF's Materials Science program is relevant to Hatch's metals & minerals practice. Apply early — Hatch is selective and reviews applications on a rolling basis. Attend info sessions if available.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA, Creo/Solidworks, Ansys Granta (materials selection — directly relevant to Hatch's metals & minerals work), NMR/FTIR (materials characterization), GD&T, PPAP. Hatch values candidates with strong materials science fundamentals and analytical thinking. Keywords: materials engineering, metallurgy, minerals processing, process optimization, CAD/FEA, quality systems, sustainability. Frame your polymer research as materials science depth. Mention any metals, ceramics, or minerals coursework.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 3 weeks. Target metals & minerals or energy practice — your Materials Engineering background is an ideal fit. Hatch values candidates who demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Prepare STAR stories about materials characterization, process optimization, and cross-functional collaboration. Hatch emphasizes safety, sustainability, and innovation — mention any relevant experience. Return-offer rate estimated at 50–60%. The Student Vacation Program is a pipeline to Hatch's full-time graduate program.
