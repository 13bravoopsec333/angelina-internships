📝 Description

Power systems and IoT semiconductors. Firmware, circuit design, and application engineering roles. Green energy and mobility focus.

✅ Eligibility

Students pursuing Electronics, Electrical Engineering, or related fields

📋 Application Process

Apply through infineon.com/careers or their ATS portal. Complete online application with resume and education details. Some positions require a cover letter. Initial screening by HR, then a technical phone screen with a team member. Selected candidates may complete 1-2 rounds of technical interviews, sometimes including a brief coding or problem-solving exercise. For semiconductor engineering roles, expect questions on circuit design, materials science, or process engineering basics.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through the Infineon careers portal, filtering for 'intern' or 'working student' roles. Infineon recruits at IEEE and materials science career fairs. They also attend semiconductor industry events. LinkedIn is effective for identifying hiring managers. If you have a specific location preference (San Jose for Silicon Valley, Austin for growing team), apply directly to that site's openings.

📄 Resume Tips

For semiconductor roles, emphasize any coursework in solid-state physics, electronics, thin films, or materials characterization. Your materials engineering background is relevant for process engineering and materials development. Highlight Ansys skills for simulation work and JMP for statistical analysis. Mention any cleanroom or fabrication experience. If you have semiconductor-specific coursework (even one class), list it prominently. For process engineering roles, emphasize analytical chemistry skills and your NMR/TLC experience. One page, technical formatting.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Infineon values technical depth in materials science and semiconductor fundamentals. Your materials engineering major is a direct fit for process engineering and materials development roles. Your Sumerlin research (NMR, TLC, chain polymerization) demonstrates hands-on analytical skills that translate to semiconductor process characterization. Ansys and JMP skills are directly applicable. Apply to multiple locations — San Jose has the most semiconductor-specific roles. Infineon values diversity of thought and cross-cultural collaboration (German company with US operations). Show interest in their SiC and GaN power semiconductor technology — it's a growth area.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with HR (20 min, behavioral + GPA), followed by a technical interview with the hiring manager or team lead (45-60 min). Questions cover semiconductor fundamentals, materials science concepts, and problem-solving. May include questions about your specific research experience and how it relates to semiconductor processes. Behavioral questions focus on teamwork, learning agility, and working in international environments. Final round may be a virtual meeting with senior team members.

🏆 Success Stories

Materials engineering students with semiconductor-relevant coursework (thin films, solid-state, materials characterization) are highly competitive. Infineon specifically recruits from materials science programs for process and device engineering internships. Students with research experience in analytical techniques (NMR, spectroscopy) are valued for process characterization roles. The company has a strong intern-to-full-time conversion rate, especially for students who demonstrate technical depth and cultural alignment.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Some postings include technical questions about your area of interest (e.g., 'Describe your experience with materials characterization techniques' or 'How does your research experience relate to semiconductor manufacturing?'). Research Infineon's key technologies (SiC power devices, MEMS sensors, automotive semiconductors) and mention specific areas that align with your skills.
