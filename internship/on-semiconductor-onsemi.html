Power semiconductor and sensor solutions. Automotive, industrial, and cloud power applications.

✅ Eligibility

📋 Application Process

1) Apply at onsemi.com/careers/students. 2) Recruiter review. 3) HR screening call. 4) Technical interview(s) with hiring manager/panel. 5) Possible 2nd round. 6) Offer. Multiple rounds typical (2-4 rounds per Glassdoor). Positions in multiple US locations plus global sites.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly at onsemi.com/careers/students. Filter for 'Internship' under Student category. onsemi has US facilities in Phoenix AZ, South Portland ME, bucharest Romania, and others. Join University Relations program for early access. Also check LinkedIn and WayUp for current postings. Internships are paid and offer meaningful work from day one.

📄 Resume Tips

Semiconductor manufacturing is materials-intensive — highlight: materials characterization (NMR, FTIR), thin films, semiconductor physics coursework if any. Your Ansys Granta is relevant for materials selection in semiconductor packaging. Mention any cleanroom experience. NMR/polymer research shows analytical skills valued in semiconductor process engineering. Creo/SolidWorks for equipment or packaging design. If you've taken any semiconductor/device physics courses, highlight them prominently.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

onsemi is a leader in power semiconductors (SiC, MOSFETs, IGBTs) — express interest in wide-bandgap semiconductors (SiC/GaN) which are hot topics. Your materials engineering background is relevant for process integration, yield engineering, and materials characterization roles. Mention interest in power electronics or automotive semiconductors (onsemi is big in EV powertrains). Apply early and follow up with University Relations contacts.
