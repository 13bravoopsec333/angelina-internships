📝 Description

Summer 2027 Intern application period open, closes October 16. Paid 10-12 week program.

✅ Eligibility

Students

📋 Application Process

1) Apply online at scaled.com/careers via the ADP portal. 2) HR/Recruiter phone screen (20 min). 3) Hiring manager video call (45 min, technical + behavioral). 4) On-site panel interview (half-day, meet the team, facility tour). 5) Background check and security clearance process (can take weeks to months). 6) Offer after clearance is granted. The process is slower due to security requirements.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly through scaled.com/careers. Scaled Composites is a subsidiary of Northrop Grumman — they build experimental aircraft. Mention specific interest in aerospace, composites, or experimental vehicle design. The 9/80 work schedule and competitive benefits make this highly sought-after. Network with Scaled employees on LinkedIn. Apply early — clearance processing takes time.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight SolidWorks/Creo and any CAD experience — Scaled designs and builds experimental aircraft from scratch. Mention your HNI experience with GD&T and prototyping. Any experience with composites, manufacturing, or hands-on fabrication is valuable. Include coursework in aerospace structures, materials, or fluid mechanics. GPA matters but hands-on skills matter more. Mention any shop/lab experience.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Scaled is extremely selective and values hands-on builders and problem-solvers. If you've built things, worked in a machine shop, or have hands-on fabrication experience, emphasize it heavily. The Mojave location is remote — show willingness to relocate for the summer. Be aware of security clearance requirements (US citizenship typically required). Network aggressively with Scaled employees.

🎤 Interview Format

2–3 rounds: phone screen → video with hiring manager → on-site half-day interview. On-site includes technical interviews with engineers, a presentation on past work, and a facility tour. Expect deep technical questions on materials, structures, and manufacturing. They value practical problem-solving over theoretical knowledge.

🏆 Success Stories

Scaled interns report working on actual experimental aircraft programs — this is not typical intern work. The company's culture is intensely engineering-driven and collaborative. Interns get hands-on experience with cutting-edge aerospace technology. Many interns convert to full-time. The remote Mojave location creates a tight-knit team culture.

📝 Supplemental Questions

No known supplemental questions on the application, but be prepared to discuss: (1) your most hands-on engineering project, (2) experience with composites or manufacturing, (3) why experimental aerospace interests you, (4) willingness to work in Mojave, CA (remote desert location).
