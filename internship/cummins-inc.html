📝 Description

Global power leader. Engineering internships in thermal & fluid systems, manufacturing, product design, and more. 4-12 month commitments available.

✅ Eligibility

Full-time enrolled student pursuing undergraduate or graduate degree at accredited US university. Minimum 2.5 GPA.

📋 Application Process

1. Apply at cummins.com/careers -> Early Careers. 2. Complete online application (resume, GPA, work authorization, location preferences, transcript upload). 3. Digital assessment (situational judgment + cognitive test, ~45 min). 4. Recruiter phone screen (20-30 min, behavioral + career goals). 5. Virtual interview with hiring manager (45-60 min). 6. Second round with team panel or cross-functional interview (45-60 min). 7. Final round may include senior leadership conversation. 8. Offers extended 2-4 weeks after final round. Cummins is very structured -- expect a thorough process.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through the careers portal. Cummins recruits heavily at Big Ten and Midwest schools (Purdue, IU, Michigan, Ohio State, Notre Dame, Rose-Holman). Attend their campus info sessions and career fairs. Cummins has a strong campus recruiting presence -- connect with recruiters early. They also attend NSBE, SWE, and SHPE conferences. Apply to multiple roles across different business units.

📄 Resume Tips

GPA 3.0+ (they verify transcript). Cummins values diversity of experience -- include any leadership, volunteer work, or extracurriculars. Highlight technical skills: CAD (SolidWorks, Creo), programming (MATLAB, Python), materials testing, GD&T. Your NMR and polymer research experience can differentiate you for R&D roles. Include any powertrain, automotive, or heavy-duty engine experience if applicable. Cummins emphasizes 'Integrity, Diversity & Inclusion, Caring, Excellence, Teamwork' -- weave these values into your resume.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Cummins is one of the more competitive industrial internships. Apply early (within first 2 weeks of posting). Complete the digital assessment carefully -- it is a real screen. Research Cummins' powertrain, hydrogen fuel cells, and battery electric technology. Show interest in both traditional and emerging technologies. Attend their 'Cummins Experience' events at target schools. Network with Cummins employees, especially in your target business unit. Follow up after every interaction.

🎤 Interview Format

2-3 rounds. Round 1: Recruiter screen (behavioral: tell me about yourself, why Cummins, career goals). Round 2: Hiring manager interview (mix of behavioral and technical). Round 3: Panel interview with team members and/or cross-functional partners. Questions focus on Cummins values and behavioral competencies. May include technical problem-solving scenarios. Dress is business casual. Virtual via Teams. Cummins uses structured behavioral interviewing -- prepare STAR stories for each of their values.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Common: 'Why Cummins?' (reference powertrain leadership, hydrogen/electric future, global impact, their values). 'Describe a time you demonstrated integrity.' 'How do you approach complex technical problems?' 'Tell me about a time you worked with a diverse team.' 'What experience do you have with engine systems or powertrain?' (if applicable). 'How do you handle competing deadlines?' 'Describe a time you made a data-driven decision.'
