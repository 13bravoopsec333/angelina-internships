📝 Description

Materials science research in Engineered Materials group (MST-7). Material design, testing, analysis. CAD experience and lab experience preferred. On-site with potential hybrid.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled undergrad, GPA 2.75+, must have completed at least one semester

📋 Application Process

1) Apply through DOE SULI program (science.osti.gov/wdts/suli) OR directly through LANL careers portal 2) For SULI: submit transcript, resume, personal statement, research interest statement, and 2 recommendation letters via ORAU portal 3) Application reviewed by program office 4) Matched with LANL mentor based on research interests 5) Notification in ~8-10 weeks after deadline. SULI deadlines: Fall term (Sept 30), Spring term (TBD). Direct hire: rolling applications via LANL careers portal. Must be US citizen or permanent resident. Competitive — ~30% acceptance rate for SULI.

🎤 Interview Format

SULI does not typically have a formal interview process. Selection is based on application materials: GPA, research experience, recommendation letters, personal statement, and research interest alignment with available mentors. Some labs may conduct a brief phone screen or video call before final selection, but most decisions are made from the application alone. If interviewed, expect questions about your research experience, analytical techniques, and career goals.
