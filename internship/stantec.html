📝 Description

Summer internships in water, energy, infrastructure, buildings. Hands-on experience with professional civil and geotechnical engineers.

✅ Eligibility

Pursuing BS in Civil Engineering or related. Within ~3 years of graduation. Min GPA 3.0 preferred.

📋 Application Process

Apply online via stantec.jobs or campus recruiting platforms (Handshake). Resume review → phone screen → interviews → contingent offer → pre-employment screening. Positions posted across buildings, energy & resources, water, infrastructure, and environmental services groups. Rolling applications — no fixed deadlines reported.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through stantec.jobs/campaigns/internship-co-op-student/jobs/ or Handshake. Stantec recruits heavily at universities — check if UF is a target school. For materials engineering, target environmental consulting or materials testing roles. Highlight FTIR, NMR, and analytical chemistry skills. Mention any environmental or sustainability coursework. Stantec is a global design firm — many roles touch materials in construction, infrastructure, and environmental remediation.

📄 Resume Tips

Stantec values problem-solving, communication, and collaboration. Highlight Sumerlin group research as demonstrating analytical skills. Include Ansys Granta for materials selection. Mention JMP for statistical analysis of experimental data. PPAP and GD&T from HNI are relevant for quality/inspection roles. Tailor resume to specific role — environmental vs. materials testing vs. quality. Stantec values diversity — mention any leadership or community involvement.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Stantec is a large global firm (28,000+ employees) with many intern positions. Apply broadly across different practice areas. The firm's environmental services group often needs materials science expertise for contamination testing, remediation materials, and water treatment. Network through ASCE or similar professional organizations. Stantec's Developing Professionals Group supports early career development.

🎤 Interview Format

Typically 1-2 rounds. Phone screen followed by behavioral/technical interview with hiring manager and team members. Questions focus on technical skills, teamwork, and project experience. May include scenario-based questions about handling project challenges. Casual to moderate formality.

🏆 Success Stories

Interviews are generally straightforward. Stantec's size means many potential placement locations across North America.
