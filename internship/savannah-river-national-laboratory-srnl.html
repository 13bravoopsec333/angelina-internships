📝 Description

10-week summer internships in national security, environmental stewardship, and energy research. Chemical engineering intern positions available. Extends up to one year.

✅ Eligibility

Full-time student at accredited 4-year institution, US citizen, STEM major

📋 Application Process

Apply through SRNL's student programs page or the DOE internships portal. SRNL is located at the Savannah River Site in Aiken, SC — a nuclear materials and environmental remediation facility. ASPIRE is SRNL's primary internship program for undergraduates. Requires: resume, transcript, statement of interest, and may require a security clearance investigation (US citizenship required). Application typically through SRNL's website or DOE portals. After application, mentors review and conduct phone/video interviews.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through SRNL's student programs page (search for current URLs as they change). Also check DOE internships portal. For materials science: SRNL focuses on nuclear materials, environmental remediation, and national security — tailor application to these areas. UF has nuclear engineering and materials science programs that may have SRNL connections. Contact UF's nuclear engineering department for introductions. Email SRNL mentors directly in materials science or chemistry groups.

📄 Resume Tips

Lead with: Materials Science/Engineering major, GPA 3.96, polymer research experience (SRNL works with nuclear waste packaging materials — polymer degradation is relevant), NMR and FTIR analytical techniques, Ansys Granta materials selection, Creo/Solidworks CAD. Highlight: any experience with radiation effects on materials, nuclear materials, environmental chemistry, or waste processing. SRNL values: security mindset, attention to detail, analytical skills. Mention Gator Blacksmithing (metals experience), Material Advantage. US citizenship is required — confirm this prominently.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) SRNL is a national security lab — US citizenship is mandatory. Confirm this in your application. 2) Security clearance process takes time — apply early. 3) Research SRNL's focus areas: nuclear materials, environmental remediation, hydrogen technology, cybersecurity. 4) Tailor your statement of interest to nuclear materials or environmental applications. 5) UF's nuclear engineering program may have SRNL contacts — ask for introductions. 6) Aiken, SC is a small town — emphasize willingness to relocate. 7) SRNL values practical problem-solving — highlight manufacturing and hands-on experience. 8) The security investigation is part of the process — be prepared for background questions.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone or video interview (20-30 min) with the potential mentor/PI. Questions focus on: research interests, understanding of nuclear materials/environmental science, security awareness, career goals. May include questions about SRNL's specific research areas. The security clearance process is separate from the interview. Technical questions about materials science fundamentals are common.

🏆 Success Stories

SRNL ASPIRE internships are less publicly discussed than other DOE labs, but students who have participated describe valuable hands-on experience with nuclear materials and environmental science. The program provides exposure to national security research. Successful candidates demonstrate interest in nuclear materials, environmental science, or national security. Students with strong analytical backgrounds and security clearance eligibility are preferred. Former interns note the unique access to specialized facilities not available at universities.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Statement of interest explaining: your interest in SRNL's research areas, what skills you bring, how the experience fits your career goals. Faculty recommendation letter may be required. Unofficial transcript. Security clearance questionnaire.
