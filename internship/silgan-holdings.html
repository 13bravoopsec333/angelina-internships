📝 Description

Equipment engineering internship at metal and plastic packaging manufacturer. Detail-oriented work in manufacturing and packaging.

✅ Eligibility

Sophomore+ students, mechanical design/engineering majors

📋 Application Process

1) Submit application online with resume and optional cover letter. 2) Phone screen with HR (15-20 min). 3) Video interview with hiring manager and team lead (45-60 min). 4) On-site interview at plant or office (half-day, includes tour and technical discussion). 5) Offer within 1 week. Silgan moves quickly — smaller HR team means faster decisions.

Apply directly through Silgan's career portal. Silgan has multiple divisions (metal containers, plastic containers, closures) — specify which division interests you. UF's proximity to Silgan's Florida operations (if any) could be an advantage.

Highlight equipment engineering, manufacturing, or maintenance experience from HNI internship. Mention PPAP experience — Silgan uses automotive-style quality systems. Include any automation or robotics exposure. GPA 3.96 is impressive; make it prominent.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Silgan values hands-on manufacturing experience — your HNI internship with GD&T and PPAP is a strong differentiator. Express interest in a specific division (metal vs. plastic containers). Mention any experience with automated production lines, metal forming, or high-speed packaging equipment. Silgan is growing through acquisitions — show you're adaptable and willing to learn new processes. The equipment engineering role involves maintaining and improving production equipment, so mechanical aptitude is key.

🎤 Interview Format

Two rounds: (1) Brief HR phone screen for logistics and basic fit. (2) Technical interview with engineering team — focus on equipment troubleshooting, manufacturing processes, and problem-solving. Silgan asks practical questions like 'Describe a time you fixed something mechanical' or 'How would you approach diagnosing a production line failure?' Less structured than large-company interviews; more conversational. They value mechanical intuition and hands-on ability.

The smaller program size means more individual attention and responsibility.
