📝 Description

10-week immersive in-person experience. World's leading provider of sustainable aluminum packaging for beverage, personal care, and household products.

✅ Eligibility

Juniors and seniors, engineering/packaging majors

📋 Application Process

Applications open on jobs.ball.com (Workday ATS), typically in August–October for the following summer. Ball calls their program 'NCA' (North Central America) Summer Engineering Internship. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major match). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Hiring manager interview (45–60 min, virtual): behavioral + technical. (5) Offer within 2–4 weeks. Ball Corporation is the world's largest aluminum can manufacturer — major manufacturing plants across the US including Aurora CO, Findlay OH, and Schaumburg IL (HQ).

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on jobs.ball.com. Also available through Handshake and university career fairs. Ball recruits at engineering schools with strong manufacturing/materials programs — UF is a target. Apply within the first 3 weeks. Ball's NCA division is the largest business unit and hires the most interns. Attend info sessions for direct recruiter contact.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA, Creo/Solidworks (CAD used in can manufacturing design), Ansys Granta (materials selection — directly relevant to aluminum alloy selection), GD&T (precision manufacturing), PPAP (quality systems — Ball uses automotive-style quality processes), NMR/FTIR (materials characterization). Ball values manufacturing engineering skills. Keywords: aluminum packaging, manufacturing processes, quality systems, materials testing, GD&T, PPAP, process optimization, sustainability (Ball emphasizes recyclability). Your HNI experience with product development and GD&T is directly transferable.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 3 weeks. Ball's NCA division is the primary hiring unit for engineering interns. Your Materials Engineering background is an ideal fit for their aluminum packaging work. Prepare STAR stories about manufacturing processes, quality systems, and product development. Ball emphasizes sustainability (aluminum is infinitely recyclable) — mention any environmental materials coursework. The internship is a pipeline to Ball's full-time Engineering Development Program. Return-offer rate estimated at 55–65%.

🎤 Interview Format

1–2 rounds. Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min) → Hiring manager virtual interview (45–60 min). Glassdoor difficulty 2.5–3.0/5. Behavioral STAR questions plus manufacturing/materials technical questions. May include questions about aluminum forming, quality systems, or process optimization. No coding tests.

No supplemental essays required. Online application includes screening questions about GPA, work authorization, location preference, and willingness to work in a manufacturing environment (rotating shifts may be required for some plant-based roles).
