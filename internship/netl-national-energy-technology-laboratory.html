📝 Description

Energy-related research internships. PIP, SULI, MLEF (Mickey Leland Energy Fellowship), and other programs. Advanced energy technology research.

✅ Eligibility

Undergrad/grad student or recent grad, US citizen or foreign national 18+

📋 Application Process

SULI: 1) Apply through the DOE SULI portal (science.energy.gov/workforce-internships-fellowships). 2) Select NETL as preferred lab. 3) Submit transcript, resume, statement of interest, and faculty recommendation. 4) Matching with a research mentor. 5) Notification ~March. Direct/Professional Internship: 1) Email a NETL research scientist whose work aligns with your interests. 2) Send resume, transcript, and brief cover letter. 3) If they have funding, they sponsor you through ORAU or a similar contractor. Timeline varies.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through SULI (January deadline) AND simultaneously email 2–3 NETL researchers whose work on polymers, materials for energy, or advanced manufacturing aligns with your background. Cold emails with specific references to their publications work well. The dual approach maximizes chances.

📄 Resume Tips

Emphasize polymer research (Sumerlin Group) and analytical skills (NMR, FTIR, TLC). DOE labs value research experience and quantitative skills. Include any GPA. Mention coursework in materials science, thermodynamics, kinetics. If you have JMP or data analysis experience, highlight it. Lab safety training is a plus.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

The single biggest factor is connecting directly with a research mentor who wants an intern and has funding. Use Google Scholar or NETL's publication database to find researchers working on polymer membranes for CO2 capture, catalysts, additive manufacturing of metals, or other energy-related materials. Send a targeted email referencing their work. SULI is competitive (~15% acceptance) but your GPA and research experience make you competitive. Apply to multiple DOE labs as backup.

🎤 Interview Format

SULI: No formal interview — selection is application-based with mentor matching. Direct/Professional Internship: Typically 1 video or phone interview with the prospective research mentor and possibly their group. Questions focus on your research experience, technical skills, and what you hope to learn. Very research-oriented — expect to discuss your NMR and polymer work in detail.

🏆 Success Stories

SULI interns at NETL frequently publish papers and present at national conferences. Many convert to full-time ORISE fellows or are recommended for graduate school. The experience is research-heavy and highly valued by graduate admissions committees. Interns report significant independence and mentorship.

📝 Supplemental Questions

SULI application requires: (1) Statement of interest (1–2 pages) describing your research interests and why NETL, (2) Faculty recommendation letter, (3) Official transcript. The statement should connect your polymer/nmr background to NETL's energy research mission.
