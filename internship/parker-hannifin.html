📝 Description

Fortune 500 motion & control. Engineering, accounting, HR, IT, marketing internships. Co-op tracks available.

✅ Eligibility

Students (seniors considered for full-time)

📋 Application Process

Apply via Parker's career portal (parkercareers.ttcportals.com). Application goes through resume screening, then a phone/video screen with HR, followed by a panel interview with the hiring team (engineers and managers). Interns with good evaluations are invited to return for additional summers. Seniors are considered for full-time positions. Posted in December for the following summer.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through parkercareers.ttcportals.com. Attend Parker's campus recruiting events — they recruit at major engineering schools. Parker has a strong internship-to-full-time pipeline (the testimonial from 'Tom, 3-time Summer Intern' illustrates this). Connect with Parker engineers on LinkedIn and mention your motion/control interests. Employee referrals are effective.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight your Ansys and FEA experience — Parker does extensive motion and control analysis. Emphasize Creo and SolidWorks CAD skills. Your GD&T knowledge from HNI is directly applicable to Parker's precision manufacturing. Mention any fluid power, hydraulics, or pneumatics coursework. Include your GPA (3.96) — Parker values academic excellence. Highlight your ability to work on the manufacturing floor as well as in design roles.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply early — Parker's internship postings typically open in fall and positions fill quickly. Target the Cleveland headquarters location for maximum exposure. Mention your interest in motion and control technologies — Parker is the global leader in this space. Emphasize your manufacturing floor experience from HNI. Connect with Parker's university recruiting team. Returning interns get priority — express interest in multiple summers.

🎤 Interview Format

Per Glassdoor, Parker interviews are mostly behavioral with some technical components. Expect questions like 'Tell me about a time you had to deal with tight deadlines' and technical questions about engineering principles. Panel format with 2–3 interviewers is common. Interview difficulty is moderate. Some positions may include a plant tour.
