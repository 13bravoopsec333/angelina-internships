📝 Description

3D-printed rocket company. Cape Canaveral launch operations. 12-week format. Terran R reusable launch vehicle. Manufacturing technology focus.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled in college/university, ITAR requirements apply

📋 Application Process

Apply through relativityspace.com/careers. Application includes resume and supplemental questions about your interest in space/additive manufacturing, technical projects, and problem-solving approach. If selected, phone screen with recruiter, then 2-3 technical interviews with engineering team members, possibly a technical assessment or coding challenge depending on role. Very competitive — Relativity is a high-growth aerospace startup. Positions open year-round. Strong preference for candidates who demonstrate first-principles thinking and passion for space.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through Relativity's careers portal. Also check LinkedIn for active postings. Relativity attends select career fairs and aerospace conferences. Apply to multiple roles (Propulsion, Structures, Manufacturing, Test). Connect with Relativity engineers on LinkedIn and mention specific projects (Terran 1, Terran R, Stargate 3D printer). Your additive manufacturing / 3D printing interest aligns with Relativity's core technology. Mention your Creo, Solidworks, and Polyworks experience.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight Creo, Solidworks, and Polyworks — Relativity builds rockets using massive 3D printers (Stargate), so CAD and metrology skills are directly applicable. Emphasize any hands-on manufacturing or prototyping experience. Your Sumerlin Polymer Research Group work is relevant — Relativity works with metal alloys and advanced materials. Mention Ansys for simulation capabilities. Your GD&T and PPAP experience from HNI shows manufacturing quality awareness. Your GPA 3.96 is competitive. Tailor resume to specific role (propulsion, structures, manufacturing, test engineering). Show passion for space and additive manufacturing in your resume summary or cover letter.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Relativity values first-principles thinking, rapid iteration, and passion for space — demonstrate these in supplemental questions and interviews. Show you've done deep research on their technology (Stargate 3D printer, Terran R). Apply to multiple roles. Relativity is growing fast — they hire aggressively for manufacturing and test roles. Your CAD + manufacturing + materials background is a strong fit. Attend aerospace conferences or events where Relativity recruits. Connect with Relativity engineers on LinkedIn. Relativity values builders — highlight any personal projects, clubs, or hands-on work.

🎤 Interview Format

3-4 rounds: (1) Recruiter phone screen (30 min), (2) Technical phone screen with engineer (45-60 min), (3) Virtual on-site with 3-4 interviews (45 min each) covering technical depth, system design, behavioral, and culture fit. Technical questions may include CAD design challenges, materials selection, manufacturing process design, or problem-solving exercises. Behavioral questions about working under pressure, iteration, and first-principles thinking. Dress business casual.
