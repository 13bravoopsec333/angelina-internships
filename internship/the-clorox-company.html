📝 Description

10-week summer internship (June-August). R&D and engineering roles. Meaningful work assignments providing practical experience. Nurturing, supportive environment.

✅ Eligibility

Students, engineering/chemistry/STEM majors

📋 Application Process

1. Apply at careers.clorox.com -> Students & Interns. 2. Complete application with resume, GPA, work authorization, and 2-3 short-answer questions about Clorox brands and career interests. 3. Digital assessment (situational judgment, ~20-30 min). 4. Recruiter phone screen (15-20 min). 5. Virtual interview with hiring manager + team (45-60 min). 6. Final round may include a case study or cross-functional panel. 7. Offer typically within 2-3 weeks.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through the careers portal. Clorox recruits at select West Coast and Big Ten schools (UC Berkeley, Stanford, Michigan, Ohio State, Purdue). Attend campus info sessions. Clorox values candidates who demonstrate consumer empathy -- mention personal experiences with their brands (Burt's Bees, Glad, Fresh Step, Liquid-Plumr). Connect with Clorox recruiters on LinkedIn and attend their diversity events.

📄 Resume Tips

GPA cutoff typically 3.0+. Clorox emphasizes innovation and sustainability -- highlight any green chemistry, sustainable materials, or polymer science coursework. Your NMR and FTIR skills are directly applicable to their product R&D. Consumer products experience (even informal) is a plus. Quantify results: 'Reduced defect rate by X%' or 'Improved process efficiency by Y%'. Include relevant software (JMP, SolidWorks, Creo). Show curiosity and learning agility.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply early and to multiple roles. Clorox values 'Brave Directness' as a core value -- show you can speak up and challenge ideas respectfully. Research their brand portfolio thoroughly and reference specific products in interviews. Demonstrate consumer empathy -- Clorox is deeply consumer-centric. Attend their 'Clorox Launch' events at target schools. Follow up with personalized thank-you emails within 24 hours. Show interest in sustainability -- Clorox has aggressive ESG goals.

🎤 Interview Format

2 rounds typical. Round 1: Recruiter screen (behavioral, career goals). Round 2: Panel or 1-on-1 with hiring manager + team. Mix of behavioral and technical questions. May include a case study (e.g., 'How would you improve the packaging for X product?'). Questions focus on Clorox values: 'Tell me about a time you were brave in sharing your opinion.' 'Describe a time you made a consumer-focused decision.' Dress is business casual (smart casual acceptable). Virtual interviews via Teams or Zoom.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Common: 'Why Clorox?' (mention specific brands, innovation pipeline, sustainability commitments). 'Describe a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.' 'What consumer product innovation impresses you and why?' 'How do you prioritize when everything feels urgent?' 'Tell us about a time you challenged the status quo.' R&D roles: 'Describe your experience with formulation or materials characterization.' 'How would you approach reformulating a product for sustainability?'
