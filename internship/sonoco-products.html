📝 Description

Manufacturing and packaging industry immersion. On-the-job learning, plant tours, leadership engagement, real-time feedback. Available in approximately 25 US locations.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled students, engineering majors

📋 Application Process

Apply through sonoco.com/careers or their ATS portal. Complete online application with resume and education details. Some roles require a cover letter. Initial screening by HR, then a phone screen with the hiring manager. Selected candidates do a technical interview (1-2 rounds) with engineering team members. For packaging roles, expect questions about materials properties, packaging design, and manufacturing processes.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through Sonoco's careers portal, filtering for 'intern' and specific locations. Sonoco attends packaging and materials science career fairs. They recruit at universities in the Southeast especially (Clemson, Georgia Tech, UF). LinkedIn is effective for identifying hiring managers. Apply to Hartsville SC for the broadest exposure to packaging engineering.

📄 Resume Tips

This is an excellent fit — emphasize materials engineering, packaging design, and manufacturing. Highlight your CAD skills (Solidworks, Creo) for packaging design and prototyping. Your materials engineering background is directly relevant — packaging is a materials-intensive field. Mention any experience with polymers, paper, or composite materials from coursework or research. HNI manufacturing experience is valuable for understanding production processes. Your Ansys skills are applicable for structural simulation of packaging. One page, showcase packaging-relevant skills.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Sonoco is one of the largest packaging companies in the world and values candidates who understand materials science as it applies to packaging. Your materials engineering background is the ideal fit. Emphasize your understanding of material properties, processing, and sustainability — packaging is increasingly focused on sustainable materials and circular economy. 🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with HR (20 min, behavioral + GPA), followed by a technical interview with the hiring manager or team lead (45-60 min). Questions focus on materials science fundamentals, packaging concepts, and your specific experience. Behavioral questions emphasize innovation, problem-solving, and working in manufacturing environments. May include a brief technical exercise or discussion of a specific packaging challenge.

🏆 Success Stories
