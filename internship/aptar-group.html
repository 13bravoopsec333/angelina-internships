📝 Description

Engineering internship supporting manufacturing processes and equipment design, focusing on injection molding and continuous improvement. Pathway to full-time roles.

✅ Eligibility

Engineering students

📋 Application Process

1. Apply at aptargroup.com/careers or through campus career portal. 2. Upload resume and complete brief application (GPA, location preference, work authorization). 3. Some roles require short-answer questions about dispensing/packaging interest. 4. Recruiter phone screen (15-20 min). 5. Virtual interview with hiring manager (30-45 min). 6. Second round with team members or technical lead (30-45 min). 7. Some engineering roles include a brief technical assessment or CAD exercise. 8. Offer within 1-2 weeks.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on aptargroup.com/careers. Aptar recruits at Midwest engineering schools (University of Illinois, Purdue, Northwestern, Marquette, Rose-Holman). Attend their campus info sessions. Connect with Aptar engineers on LinkedIn. If you're at a non-target school, reach out to recruiters directly. Aptar values hands-on experience -- highlight any prototyping or manufacturing exposure.

📄 Resume Tips

GPA typically 3.0+. Emphasize mechanical design skills (SolidWorks, Creo -- both directly applicable), GD&T knowledge, and any packaging or dispensing experience. Your Creo experience from HNI is a strong match. Aptar makes precision dispensing systems -- highlight any experience with tolerances, injection molding, or mechanical design. Include any polymer or materials science coursework. Show you understand manufacturing processes (injection molding, assembly, testing).

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Aptar is a mid-size company where individual contributions matter -- emphasize your ability to work independently and take ownership. Research their product lines (beauty, personal care, food/beverage dispensing, pharma). Mention specific Aptar products you find innovative. Show interest in the intersection of engineering and consumer experience. Apply to multiple roles across their US sites. Follow up with recruiters after applying. Aptar values global mindset -- mention any international experience or language skills.

🎤 Interview Format

2 rounds typical. Round 1: Recruiter phone screen (behavioral + GPA verification). Round 2: Virtual or on-site interview with hiring manager + engineering team. Technical questions about CAD, GD&T, materials, manufacturing processes. May include a whiteboard design exercise or CAD walkthrough of a past project. Behavioral questions about teamwork, problem-solving. Dress is business casual. Aptar interviews tend to be conversational and technical.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Common: 'Why Aptar?' (mention their leadership in dispensing, innovation in sustainable packaging, global reach). 'Describe a mechanical design project you've worked on.' 'How do you approach tolerance analysis?' 'What experience do you have with injection molding or polymer processing?' 'Tell me about a time you solved a technical problem creatively.' 'How do you handle working on multiple projects simultaneously?'
