📝 Description

Aerospace repair group. Research, drawings, analysis for aircraft repairs and testing equipment. FL presence.

✅ Eligibility

Students

📋 Application Process

Apply through HEICO careers portal (careers.heico.com). HEICO is a mid-size aerospace/defense/electronics manufacturer — less formal than large corporations. Standard application: resume + cover letter. Smaller company means faster hiring cycles. Interviews typically conducted by hiring manager and team lead. HEICO makes replacement parts for aircraft engines and components — materials testing, failure analysis, and manufacturing are key areas.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly through careers.heico.com. HEICO is smaller, so referral from UF alumni or LinkedIn connections carries significant weight. Check UF Handshake for postings. HEICO recruits at select engineering career fairs. For Hollywood FL location, reach out to UF alumni working there via LinkedIn. Cold-email the engineering manager for the specific division you're interested in.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Creo and Solidworks proficiency (HEICO uses both for engine component design), GD&T expertise from HNI internship, PPAP experience, materials selection knowledge (Ansys Granta), FTIR and materials characterization techniques. Emphasize manufacturing experience and quality systems knowledge. HEICO values practical hands-on skills over research. Mention Materials Advantage student org involvement. The blacksmithing hobby demonstrates metalworking passion and hands-on fabrication skills — include it.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) HEICO is a Defense/ITAR-restricted company — ensure you can work with controlled technical data (US citizen required for many positions). 2) Research HEICO's specific divisions: HEICO Aerospace (engine parts), HEICO Electronic Technologies (RF/microwave components). 3) Tailor resume to the specific division. 4) Contact UF alumni at HEICO via LinkedIn — small company referrals matter a LOT. 5) Mention your PPAP experience — HEICO supplies to major OEMs and PPAP knowledge is directly relevant. 6) Apply early — smaller companies fill positions faster.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screening followed by in-person or video interview with engineering team. Technical questions about CAD software, materials properties, manufacturing processes. Behavioral questions about teamwork and problem-solving. May include facility tour. Less formal than big-company interviews.
