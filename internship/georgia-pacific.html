Mechanical engineering internship at Internal Engineering team in Atlanta. Part of Koch companies. Manufacturing of building products, tissue, packaging, paper. Currently hiring for Summer 2027.

✅ Eligibility

Engineering students (mechanical, chemical, electrical, industrial)

📋 Application Process

Apply via koch.avature.net (Koch Industries careers portal, parent company). Online application with resume. Recruiter screen → hiring manager interview → site visit. Must be eligible for full-time employment by Summer 2027. Hiring for summer typically opens in spring.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Koch Industries' Avature careers portal (Georgia-Pacific is a Koch company). Attend campus recruiting events at universities with strong engineering programs. Network through engineering student organizations.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight manufacturing and process engineering experience. Emphasize any building products or materials processing knowledge. Include quality systems and safety experience. Mention CAD skills (SolidWorks, Creo). Show understanding of manufacturing operations and continuous improvement.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Georgia-Pacific is one of the world's leading manufacturers of building products. Emphasize manufacturing experience and process improvement skills. Show understanding of building products industry. Highlight safety consciousness (critical in manufacturing). Apply early and demonstrate willingness to work at manufacturing facilities nationwide.

🎤 Interview Format

Behavioral interview using STAR method. Technical questions related to manufacturing engineering. Questions about safety and compliance. May include a site tour and team meetings. Focus on Koch's MBM (Market Based Management) philosophy.

Georgia-Pacific interns participate in the Koch Intern Program for professional development. Previous interns have worked on process efficiency, equipment reliability, and quality assurance. Strong candidates receive full-time offers. The program emphasizes both technical and leadership development.
