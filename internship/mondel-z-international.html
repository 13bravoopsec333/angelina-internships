📝 Description

10-12 week summer internship in engineering, R&D, supply chain. Brands include Oreo, Cadbury, Sour Patch Kids. Applications expected to open ~September 2026 for Summer 2027.

✅ Eligibility

Rising juniors/seniors, STEM majors

📋 Application Process

Apply through mondelezinternational.com/careers or their Workday ATS. Complete online application with resume, transcript, and education details. The Taste the Future program has a structured timeline with cohort-based recruiting. Initial screening by campus recruiting team, then a phone screen with HR. Selected candidates do a 30-minute video interview with the hiring manager, followed by a final round that may include a plant visit or panel interview. Strong emphasis on GPA (3.0+ minimum) and engineering fundamentals.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through Mondelēz's careers portal and through university career services. Mondelēz attends engineering career fairs at universities nationwide. They also host information sessions and campus events specifically for the Taste the Future program. Networking with Mondelēz engineers through professional societies is effective. Apply through Handshake if your university uses it.

📄 Resume Tips

For food manufacturing engineering, emphasize coursework in process engineering, materials science, and quality systems. Your materials engineering background is relevant for packaging materials, food safety, and process optimization. Highlight any manufacturing experience from HNI (PPAP, quality processes are transferable). Mention CAD skills for equipment design and JMP for statistical process control. If you have any food science or chemistry coursework, mention it. One page, professional formatting.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Mondelēz values candidates who understand manufacturing processes and quality systems — your HNI internship with PPAP and GD&T is directly relevant. Your materials engineering background is applicable to packaging materials and process engineering. The Taste the Future program is designed as a pipeline to full-time manufacturing and engineering roles. Apply to multiple locations — manufacturing plants offer more hands-on experience than headquarters. Show genuine interest in food manufacturing and how materials science applies to food production and packaging. GPA of 3.0+ is sufficient — they weight manufacturing interest and practical experience highly.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with HR (20 min, behavioral + GPA), followed by a 30-minute video interview with the hiring manager. Questions focus on engineering fundamentals, manufacturing interest, and teamwork. Behavioral questions emphasize problem-solving in production environments and attention to detail. Final round may be a plant visit or panel interview with engineering team members. The tone is professional but friendly.

🏆 Success Stories

Engineering students with manufacturing or quality experience are strong candidates. Mondelēz specifically values candidates who understand production environments and quality systems. Students from state schools with good GPAs and relevant project experience are very competitive. The Taste the Future program emphasizes hands-on plant experience and exposure to food manufacturing operations. Many interns convert to full-time engineering roles, especially in manufacturing and quality.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Some postings ask about manufacturing experience, specific engineering interests (process, quality, packaging, maintenance), and why Mondelēz specifically. Research Mondelēz's brands (Oreo, Cadbury, Trident) and manufacturing operations. Mention specific aspects of food manufacturing that interest you — quality control, process optimization, packaging innovation. If you have any food or consumer products experience, describe it.
