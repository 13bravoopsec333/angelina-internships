📝 Description

Optical transceivers, photonics, laser technology. R&D and engineering roles with mentorship and professional development.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled Bachelor's students (rising junior or above required for most roles)

📋 Application Process

1) Submit online application with resume and transcript. 2) HR phone screen (20 min). 3) Technical video interview with hiring manager (45-60 min). 4) Final round on-site or virtual interview with team (1-2 hours, includes technical discussion). 5) Offer within 2-3 weeks. Coherent's process emphasizes technical fit for photonics and materials roles.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Coherent's career portal — search for 'intern' or 'student' positions. Saxonburg, PA headquarters and Durham, NC facility have the most openings. Coherent recruits at universities with optics and photonics programs — if UF has optics research, mention it. LinkedIn outreach to Coherent engineers in photonics or materials can help. Mention your interest in laser technology or optical materials in your application.

📄 Resume Tips

Coherent makes lasers, optical components, and photonic devices — highlight any experience with optics, photonics, or laser systems. Your Materials Engineering background is relevant for crystal growth, thin film deposition, and optical materials. Mention FTIR experience — Coherent uses spectroscopic techniques. Include any experience with lasers, optical alignment, or precision measurement. GPA 3.96 is excellent — emphasize it. Mention any cleanroom or semiconductor fabrication experience.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Coherent is a leader in photonics — show you understand their technology and applications (industrial laser processing, telecommunications, medical devices). Your analytical skills (NMR, FTIR) demonstrate the precision Coherent values. Mention any experience with optical systems, lasers, or precision manufacturing. Coherent values innovation — highlight creative solutions from your research. The photonics industry is growing rapidly — show excitement about the field. UF's materials science program likely has optics-related research — mention any connections.

🎤 Interview Format

Two to three rounds: (1) HR phone screen with behavioral questions. (2) Technical video interview with hiring manager — questions about optics, photonics, and materials science fundamentals. (3) Final round with team — may include a technical presentation or problem-solving exercise. Coherent values technical depth in optics and materials. Expect questions about laser physics, optical materials, and thin film technology. Less formal than KLA but still technically rigorous.

🏆 Success Stories

Coherent interns work on laser systems, optical components, and photonic device development. The program includes technical mentorship and exposure to cutting-edge photonics technology. Conversion rate is moderate to strong (50-60%). Past interns have become optical engineers, materials scientists, and product development engineers. Coherent's acquisition of II-VI expanded their technology portfolio significantly. The photonics industry offers diverse career paths from R&D to manufacturing.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Common supplemental questions: 'What is your experience with optics, photonics, or laser systems?', 'Describe a materials characterization project you've worked on.', 'How do your materials engineering skills apply to photonic devices?', 'What interests you about laser technology?', 'Describe your experience with cleanroom or precision manufacturing.', 'How do you approach technical problem-solving?' Coherent may also ask about your interest in specific product lines or applications.
