📝 Description

Berkshire Hathaway subsidiary specializing in investment castings for aerospace. Engineering interns work on turbine blade manufacturing, process improvement, and quality engineering. A deep niche in investment casting and superalloy materials. 3-6 month co-ops available.

✅ Eligibility

Students (minimum second year, seeking Bachelor's degree)

📋 Application Process

1) Submit online application with resume and transcript. 2) HR phone screen (20 min). 3) Technical video interview with hiring manager (45-60 min). 4) Final round on-site at Portland, OR or other facility (half-day, includes team interviews and facility tour). 5) Background check required. Offer within 2-3 weeks. PCC's process emphasizes technical fit for aerospace manufacturing.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through PCC's career portal — search for 'intern' or 'co-op' positions. Portland, OR headquarters and other casting facilities have the most openings. PCC recruits at universities with strong metallurgy and materials science programs — check if UF has aerospace materials connections. LinkedIn outreach to PCC engineers in casting, forging, or metallurgy can help. Mention your interest in aerospace manufacturing in your application.

📄 Resume Tips

PCC makes precision castings and forgings for aerospace — highlight any experience with metallurgy, casting, forging, or metalworking. Your Materials Engineering background is directly relevant for alloy development, heat treatment, and materials testing. Mention any experience with metallography, tensile testing, or failure analysis. Include any experience with GD&T and precision measurement. GPA 3.96 is excellent — emphasize it. Mention any experience with aerospace materials or manufacturing.

🎤 Interview Format

Two to three rounds: (1) HR phone screen with behavioral questions. (2) Technical video interview with hiring manager — questions about metallurgy, casting, and materials science. (3) Final round on-site with team — includes technical discussion, facility tour, and behavioral questions. PCC values technical depth in metallurgy and manufacturing. Expect questions about alloy properties, casting defects, and heat treatment. Less formal than semiconductor interviews but technically rigorous.

🏆 Success Stories

PCC interns work on casting process optimization, alloy development, quality engineering, and manufacturing improvement projects. The program includes exposure to multiple casting facilities and processes. Conversion rate is strong (60-70%).
