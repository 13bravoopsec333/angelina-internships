📝 Description

World's largest independent R&D organization. Materials research, defense technologies, energy, and health. Interns work alongside top experts on cutting-edge research. Less known than national labs but operates similar work. Great for materials characterization and advanced R&D.

✅ Eligibility

Students at all stages

📋 Application Process

Apply online at jobs.battelle.org. Upload resume and complete application. If applying for programming roles, may include HackerRank challenge. Recruiter screen → phone interviews (1-2 rounds) → in-person interview (technical) → offer. Average hiring time: 32 days. Rolling applications — no fixed deadlines. Connect on Handshake or attend university recruiting events.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply online at jobs.battelle.org/internships-and-co-ops/ or through Handshake. Attend university recruiting events if Battelle visits UF. Tailor resume to specific internship posting — use keywords from job description. Battelle operates INL and other national labs — applying to Battelle is similar to applying to national labs. For materials engineering, target advanced materials, biology, chemistry, or data science divisions. Highlight Ansys Granta, FTIR, NMR, Solidworks.

📄 Resume Tips

Battelle recruiters explicitly advise: 'Use keywords from the job posting in your resume.' Match your skills to the required/preferred qualifications in the posting. Highlight research experience from Sumerlin group — Battelle values 'engaged, critical thinkers.' Include Python, MATLAB, or other programming skills. Mention PPAP/GD&T from HNI for quality/manufacturing roles. Battelle looks for problem-solving, communication, collaboration, technology, and leadership.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Battelle is the world's largest independent R&D organization — many internship opportunities across diverse fields. The company reinvests profits for societal benefit — align your application with their mission. Battelle operates multiple national labs (INL, PNNL, ORNL, etc.) — an internship at Battelle HQ can lead to national lab opportunities. The company values 'diverse teams' — emphasize any multicultural or interdisciplinary experience. Apply to multiple postings to increase chances.

🎤 Interview Format

2-3 rounds: recruiter screen → phone/video interviews (behavioral) → in-person interview (technical). Casual and friendly atmosphere. Focus on work ethic, personality, and technical capabilities. Be ready to discuss specific projects and examples. Questions about teamwork, problem-solving, and interest in Battelle's mission. Technical questions for engineering roles.
