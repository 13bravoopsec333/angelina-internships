📝 Description

Data analytics and technology internship. Work across multiple domains to solve real-world challenges. KBR's intern program treats interns like full-time employees with hands-on project experience.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled in college/university

📋 Application Process

Apply through kbr.com/careers or their Workday ATS. Complete online application with resume, education, GPA, and security clearance status. Some positions require US citizenship or eligibility for clearance. Initial screening by HR, followed by a phone interview with the hiring manager. Selected candidates may do a panel interview with the project team. Background check and security clearance investigation required for defense-related roles.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Directly through KBR careers portal. Search 'intern' or 'FIU' if there's a specific university partnership. KBR recruits heavily at military-affiliated and engineering career fairs. If there's a FIU-specific partnership, apply through the university's career services portal as well. Networking with KBR engineers at events or through FIU career services is valuable.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight any defense or government-adjacent experience. Your materials engineering background is relevant for structural analysis and materials qualification roles. Mention CAD skills (Solidworks, Creo) and analytical tools (JMP). If you have any security clearance or military family connections, note them. Emphasize precision, documentation skills, and comfort with regulated environments — defense work requires meticulous record-keeping. One page, professional formatting. Avoid any information about specific security clearances you cannot discuss.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

KBR values reliability, technical competence, and comfort working in structured, compliance-heavy environments. Your HNI internship with PPAP and GD&T demonstrates you understand quality processes and documentation — critical in defense work. Your materials engineering background is directly applicable to materials qualification and testing roles. Being at UF (nearby Florida university) is an advantage for location. Apply early and demonstrate willingness to work in government/defense settings. If you have any DoD-relevant coursework or projects, highlight them.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with HR (20-30 min, behavioral, GPA verification, citizenship status), followed by a technical/behavioral interview with the hiring manager (30-45 min). Questions focus on technical fundamentals, comfort with structured processes, ability to follow procedures precisely, and teamwork. For defense roles, there may be questions about handling sensitive information and working in secure environments. Panel interviews are possible but less common for interns.

🏆 Success Stories

Engineering students from Florida universities (UF, FIU, UCF) are frequently hired into KBR internships. The company specifically targets local talent for defense engineering roles. Materials and mechanical engineering students with quality assurance experience (like PPAP from HNI) are strong candidates. Many interns convert to full-time positions, especially those who demonstrate comfort with the compliance and documentation requirements of defense work.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Some postings ask about US citizenship/clearance eligibility, willingness to work on government contracts, and any prior experience with defense or regulated industries. Answer honestly — clearance eligibility is a hard requirement for many roles. If you have no defense experience, frame your quality assurance background as preparation for compliance-heavy work.
