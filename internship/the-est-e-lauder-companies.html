📝 Description

R&D and Packaging STEM Summer Internship Program. 12-week program blending cosmetic industry overview with real-world engineering experience. Also Lauder Summer Internship and CEO Presidential Internship.

✅ Eligibility

Undergraduate and graduate students, STEM majors

📋 Application Process

1) Apply online via ELC careers portal 2) Online assessment (behavioral/personality, 20-30 min) 3) HR phone screen (30 min) 4) Virtual panel interview with hiring team (1-2 hours) 5) Possible final round with director 6) Offer. Structured process with clear timelines. ELC hires heavily for summer internships.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly through ELC careers portal. Check for positions in supply chain, manufacturing, or R&D that align with materials engineering. ELC values candidates who show genuine passion for the beauty industry — mention specific products or brands. Network with ELC recruiters on LinkedIn. Apply early — their internship deadlines are typically in fall for summer positions.

📄 Resume Tips

For ELC, emphasize: (1) Analytical skills — NMR, FTIR, JMP for product formulation and quality control. (2) Polymer research from Sumerlin group — directly relevant to cosmetics packaging and formulation. (3) CAD skills (Creo/Solidworks) for packaging design and manufacturing. (4) Jira/project management experience. (5) Any coursework in polymers, coatings, surface chemistry, or materials characterization. Tailor resume to show materials science applied to consumer products.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

ELC is a global beauty conglomerate (Estée Lauder, Clinique, MAC, etc.) — materials engineering is highly relevant for: packaging design, product formulation, manufacturing processes, quality control. Angelina's polymer research (chain polymerization) and analytical skills (NMR, FTIR) are directly applicable. Her HNI experience in product development and PPAP shows she understands manufacturing quality. Mention specific ELC brands or products she uses/knows. Apply to multiple departments (R&D, supply chain, packaging engineering).

🎤 Interview Format

Online behavioral assessment → HR phone screen → Virtual panel interview (2-4 team members, mix of behavioral and technical) → possible final round. Expect questions about interest in the beauty industry, problem-solving approach, teamwork, and how materials science applies to consumer products. STAR format for behavioral questions.

🏆 Success Stories

ELC internship programs are well-structured with mentorship, project ownership, and networking opportunities. Many interns receive full-time return offers. The company values diversity and innovation. Interns work on real products that reach consumers. ELC has a strong manufacturing presence in the US (Melville NY, Independence KY) where materials engineering skills are in high demand.
