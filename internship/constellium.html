📝 Description

Major aluminum rolled products manufacturer. 1,250+ employees at Muscle Shoals alone. Internships in aluminum manufacturing, rolling, and recycling. A less obvious alternative to Alcoa/Arconic — focused specifically on aluminum sheet for packaging and automotive.

✅ Eligibility

Students in engineering or materials science

📋 Application Process

Apply via jobs.constellium.com. Online application with resume upload. Recruiter phone screen → technical interview → site visit. Hiring aligned with academic calendar (summer internships posted Jan-Mar).

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through constellium.com/careers job search portal. Check for internship postings at university career fairs (Constellium recruits at top materials/metallurgical engineering programs). LinkedIn networking with Constellium engineers.

📄 Resume Tips

Emphasize materials science coursework (especially metallurgy, aluminum alloys). Highlight any manufacturing or process engineering experience. Mention GD&T and CAD proficiency. Show understanding of aluminum processing (rolling, extrusion, heat treatment). Quantify any project outcomes.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Constellium is the world's leading aluminum company for rolled and extruded products. Emphasize materials characterization experience (relevant to their quality labs). Show interest in sustainability (aluminum recycling is core to their business). Mention any metals/mechanical testing experience. Network at ASM or TMS conferences. Understand the automotive/aerospace aluminum market.

🎤 Interview Format

Technical interview focusing on materials science fundamentals. Behavioral questions about teamwork and problem-solving. May include a facility tour and meeting with potential team. For manufacturing roles, questions about process engineering and continuous improvement.

🏆 Success Stories

Constellium values engineering students who can work in industrial manufacturing settings. Previous interns have worked on process optimization, quality improvement, and new alloy development. Strong candidates demonstrate both technical knowledge and ability to work in team-oriented manufacturing environments.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Application may include: What interests you about aluminum and its applications? Describe a materials characterization project. How do you approach problem-solving in a manufacturing environment?
