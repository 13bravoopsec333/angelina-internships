📝 Description

Summer internship with meaningful work assignments. Introduction to corporate culture, networking with executives and leaders. Infrastructure projects worldwide.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled in ABET-accredited university. Civil, Mechanical, Chemical, Electrical Engineering programs.

📋 Application Process

Applications open on bv.com/careers (Workday ATS), typically in August–September for the following summer. Rolling review. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major match, keywords). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Hiring manager interview (45–60 min, virtual): behavioral STAR + technical discussion. (5) Some positions may include a second interview with team members. (6) Offer within 2–4 weeks. Black & Veatch operates across energy, water, telecommunications, and data centers — internships are posted by business line. The Overland Park, KS headquarters is the primary hub for engineering internships.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on bv.com/careers (Workday portal). Also available on Handshake and LinkedIn. Apply within the first 2–3 weeks of posting. Black & Veatch recruits heavily at Big 12 and Midwest engineering schools — UF is a target school for their Environmental and Water business lines. Attend info sessions and career fairs for direct recruiter contact. Referrals from current employees help expedite review.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA, Creo/Solidworks, Ansys Granta (materials selection), GD&T, PPAP (quality systems), NMR/FTIR. Black & Veatch values candidates with strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Keywords: engineering consulting, project management, quality systems, materials selection, environmental engineering, infrastructure, CAD/FEA. Frame your HNI internship experience as product development in a manufacturing environment — relevant to B&V's industrial consulting. Emphasize any environmental or water-related coursework for those business lines.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 2–3 weeks. Target the business line that best fits your background: Environmental (NMR/FTIR analytical skills), Water (materials engineering), or Advanced Manufacturing (HNI experience). Black & Veatch values teamwork and cross-functional collaboration — prepare STAR stories that demonstrate these. The company has a strong culture of safety and quality — mention any safety awareness or quality system experience. Return-offer rate is estimated at 50–65%. Overland Park is the primary internship location with housing assistance available.
