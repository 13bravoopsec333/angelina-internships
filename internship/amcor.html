📝 Description

Packaging engineering internship at global packaging leader. Innovative, sustainable packaging solutions across food, beverage, pharmaceutical, medical, personal care sectors.

✅ Eligibility

Engineering students

📋 Application Process

Applications open on amcor.com/careers or the Amcor Workday portal, typically in August–October for the following summer. Rolling review. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Hiring manager interview (45–60 min, virtual): behavioral + technical. (5) Offer within 2–4 weeks. Amcor is a global packaging company with US operations across flexible and rigid packaging. Major locations include Oshkosh WI, Bellefontaine OH, and Fayetteville AR.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on amcor.com/careers or through LinkedIn. Also available on Handshake. Amcor is a global company with ~44,000 employees — competition is moderate. Apply within the first 3 weeks. UF's Materials Science program is relevant to Amcor's packaging materials focus. Attend career fairs and any Amcor info sessions.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA, Creo/Solidworks, Ansys Granta (materials selection — relevant to packaging material selection), GD&T, PPAP (quality systems), NMR/FTIR (polymer characterization — directly relevant to packaging materials). Amcor values polymer science and packaging materials knowledge. Keywords: packaging engineering, polymer science, materials testing, quality systems, sustainability, flexible packaging, rigid packaging. Your Sumerlin Polymer Research experience (chain polymerization) is directly relevant to Amcor's polymer-based packaging work. Mention any polymer science or packaging coursework.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 3 weeks. Your polymer research background and analytical skills (NMR, FTIR) are a strong fit for Amcor's packaging materials work. Prepare STAR stories about polymer characterization, materials testing, and quality systems. Amcor emphasizes sustainability in packaging — mention any sustainable materials coursework or projects. Return-offer rate estimated at 50–60%. The internship may rotate across different packaging divisions.
