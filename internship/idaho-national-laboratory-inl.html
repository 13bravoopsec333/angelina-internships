📝 Description

Research in nuclear energy, national security, and critical infrastructure. Materials science, chemical, and mechanical engineering projects. 10-16 week terms.

✅ Eligibility

Enrolled full-time undergrad, GPA 3.0+, authorized to work in US

📋 Application Process

Apply online via INL Intern Portal (Oracle Cloud) or DOE SULI portal. Upload resume, transcripts, class schedule. May require research interest statement. After submission, mentors scan the talent pool continuously. Average 21 days from application to hire. SULI has fixed deadlines (Spring: Sep 30; Summer: ~Jan). INL direct is rolling talent-pipeline model — apply ASAP when postings refresh in fall.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply the week INL direct postings refresh on the Oracle Cloud portal (~Sep–Oct for summer). Also apply through DOE SULI as a parallel track. Tailor your resume to the specific track (Nuclear Engineering, Clean Energy, Computational Science, etc.). Mention Ansys/Creo/Solidworks and materials characterization experience (FTIR, NMR).

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight materials engineering skills: Ansys Granta, FTIR, NMR, Solidworks/Creo. Emphasize GPA 3.96. Include polymer research group experience (chain polymerization, TLC). For nuclear/clean energy tracks, mention any coursework in materials science, thermodynamics, or nuclear fundamentals. Quantify HNI internship impact (PPAP, GD&T work). 3.0 GPA minimum required — Angelina exceeds this significantly.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply early in the rolling cycle (first week of postings). Write a strong research interest statement matching a specific INL division. Reference specific INL research areas (advanced nuclear fuels, battery storage, hydrogen, critical minerals). Network at career fairs if INL visits UF. Apply to both direct INL and DOE SULI simultaneously. International students welcome with CPT.

🎤 Interview Format

Single 30-minute virtual call with potential research mentor. Behavioral and technical mix — ask about your research interests, relevant coursework, and why INL. Per Glassdoor intern reviews: straightforward, focused on fit with mentor's project. No panel interviews reported for internships.
