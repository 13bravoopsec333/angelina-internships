Emphasize: HVAC/climate systems knowledge if any, thermal management, materials durability, corrosion resistance. JMP statistical analysis for process optimization. Mention lean manufacturing or continuous improvement. Trane values sustainability — mention any environmental consciousness.

1) Research Trane Technologies' product lines: HVAC systems, climate controls, sustainable building solutions. 2) Their 'Gigaton Challenge' to reduce carbon emissions by 1 billion metric tons by 2030 is a key initiative — align your interests with sustainability. 3) Miramar, FL and Maitland, FL are Florida locations — mention strong interest in staying in Florida. 4) Trane values diversity and inclusion — emphasize leadership and community involvement. 5) Apply through their talent community for personalized job recommendations. 6) Review their application tips and interview best practices page before interviewing. 7) The company's 2030 commitments (carbon neutral operations, zero waste) are worth researching.

May include facility tour for in-person interviews. Questions about HVAC systems and climate solutions are common. The interview is generally less formal than aerospace companies.
