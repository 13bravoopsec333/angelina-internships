📝 Description

12-week summer internship. Steel processing and metals services.

✅ Eligibility

Students

📋 Application Process

Applications open on worthingtonenterprises.com/careers (Oracle Cloud ATS — Worthington Steel is a subsidiary of Worthington Enterprises), typically in August–October for the following summer. Process: (1) Apply online with resume via Oracle Cloud portal. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major match, keywords). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Hiring manager interview (45–60 min, virtual or on-site in Columbus OH): behavioral STAR + technical discussion. (5) Offer within 2–4 weeks. Worthington Steel operates steel processing and distribution facilities across North America. The company focuses on carbon flat-rolled steel, metal framing, and steel deck products. Columbus OH headquarters is the primary hub for engineering and operations internships.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on worthingtonenterprises.com/careers — filter for Worthington Steel positions. Also available on LinkedIn. Apply within the first 2–3 weeks. Worthington Steel is a major steel processor with strong relationships with engineering schools in the Midwest and Southeast — UF is a viable target. Attend any Worthington Steel info sessions or career fairs for direct recruiter access. Referrals from current employees help expedite review.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA, Creo/Solidworks (CAD for steel processing and product design), Ansys Granta (materials selection — directly relevant to steel alloy selection), GD&T from HNI (precision manufacturing), PPAP (quality systems — critical in steel processing), NMR/FTIR (materials characterization — relevant to steel composition analysis). Keywords: steel processing, flat-rolled steel, metal framing, manufacturing engineering, quality systems, process optimization, materials science, metallurgy. Your HNI internship with GD&T and PPAP is directly transferable to steel processing quality control.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 2–3 weeks. Worthington Steel specifically values candidates with materials science and metallurgy knowledge — your Materials Engineering background is ideal. Prepare STAR stories about manufacturing processes, quality systems, and materials characterization. Worthington Steel emphasizes safety above all — mention any safety awareness or training. The internship is a pipeline to Worthington Steel's full-time engineering roles. Return-offer rate estimated at 50–60%.

🎤 Interview Format

1–2 rounds. Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min) → Hiring manager virtual or on-site interview (45–60 min). Glassdoor difficulty 2.5–3.0/5. Behavioral STAR questions plus technical discussion about steel processing, materials science, and manufacturing. Questions focus on teamwork, problem-solving, interest in steel industry, and why Worthington Steel.

🏆 Success Stories

Worthington Steel is a subsidiary of Worthington Enterprises (NYSE: WOR). The company operates steel processing plants across the US and Canada. Interns report hands-on experience with steel processing operations, exposure to multiple plant locations, and meaningful project work. Worthington Steel values candidates who understand the steel industry and can contribute to process improvement projects. The company is actively investing in sustainability and recycling — 96% of steel is made from recycled scrap metals.

📝 Supplemental Questions

No supplemental essays required. Online application includes screening questions about GPA, work authorization, location preference (Columbus OH), and willingness to work in a manufacturing environment. Some positions may ask about shift availability or willingness to travel to plant locations.
