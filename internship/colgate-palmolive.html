📝 Description

Engineering internship focusing on process, packaging, automation, and project management. Work alongside talented teams at a truly global consumer products company.

✅ Eligibility

Students with 3.0+ GPA, engineering/science majors

📋 Application Process

1. Apply at careers.colgatepalmolive.com (select Students & Graduates). 2. Complete online application including resume, GPA, work authorization, and several behavioral/situational short-answer questions (2-3 questions, 200-word limit each). 3. If selected, complete a digital assessment (situational judgment test, ~30 min). 4. Recruiter phone screen (20-30 min, behavioral + career goals). 5. Virtual interview with hiring manager and team (1-2 rounds, 45-60 min each). 6. Some R&D roles include a technical presentation or case study. 7. Super Day or final round may involve meeting senior leadership. 8. Offers extended 2-4 weeks after final round.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply via the careers portal -- this is the primary channel. Colgate heavily recruits at target schools (Rutgers, Cornell, MIT, Georgia Tech, Penn State). Attend campus info sessions and career fairs where Colgate recruiters are present. Network with Colgate employees on LinkedIn, especially alumni from your university. Colgate values diversity -- mention any multicultural or global experience.

📄 Resume Tips

Colgate screens for 3.0+ GPA. Emphasize analytical skills (JMP, statistical analysis), lab techniques (NMR, FTIR -- directly relevant to their consumer products R&D), and any consumer products or packaging experience. Your Sumerlin Polymer Research experience is highly relevant -- Colgate does extensive polymer science for toothbrush handles, packaging, etc. Include leadership roles and community involvement. Use action verbs and quantify achievements.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Colgate's internship program is competitive (~2-3% acceptance). Apply early (within first week of posting). Complete the situational judgment assessment carefully -- it tests alignment with Colgate's values (Caring, Global Teamwork, Continuous Improvement). Research their brands (Colgate, Palmolive, Hill's, Speed Stick) and mention specific product innovations you admire. Attend their 'Colgate in Action' campus events. Follow up with recruiters after career fairs. Show global mindset -- Colgate operates in 200+ countries.

🎤 Interview Format

2-3 rounds typically. Round 1: Recruiter phone screen (behavioral). Round 2: Virtual panel interview with hiring manager + 2-3 team members. Mix of behavioral ('Tell me about a time...') and role-specific technical questions. R&D roles may require a 10-15 minute presentation on a past research project. Round 3 (for some roles): Senior leadership conversation or Super Day event. Questions focus on Colgate's Leadership Principles: 'Give constructive feedback,' 'Build trust through teamwork,' 'Act with urgency.' Business casual dress.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Key questions: 'Why Colgate-Palmolive?' (reference their Purpose-driven mission, sustainability goals, global reach). 'Describe a time you showed initiative on a project.' 'How do you handle working with people from different backgrounds?' 'What does innovation mean to you?' 'Tell us about a time you failed and what you learned.' R&D-specific: 'Describe your experience with analytical instrumentation (NMR, FTIR, chromatography).' 'How would you approach developing a new consumer product?'
