📝 Description

Engineering internships and co-ops in nuclear, mechanical, civil, chemical, and electrical engineering. 10-12 week summer sessions. Work on real-world power generation and distribution projects.

✅ Eligibility

Pursuing ABET-accredited engineering degree, GPA 2.75+ (3.0 preferred), must not graduate before Aug 2027

📋 Application Process

Apply via dukeenergy.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com. Online application with resume. Recruiter screen → hiring manager interview → team meetings. Valid driver's license required. Hiring for following summer typically opens in fall semester. Co-op rotations available.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Duke Energy's Workday careers portal. Attend Duke Energy campus recruiting events at UF (they actively recruit at Florida universities). Network with Duke Energy engineers through IEEE or engineering student organizations.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight electrical/materials engineering coursework relevant to utilities. Emphasize any power systems or infrastructure experience. Mention Ansys/FEA skills (relevant to structural analysis of power equipment). Show understanding of power generation, transmission, and distribution. Include any safety or compliance experience.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Duke Energy actively recruits at UF and Florida universities. Your location gives you a strong advantage. Emphasize reliability engineering and equipment analysis skills. Show understanding of utility industry challenges (grid modernization, renewable energy integration). Mention HNI internship experience with quality systems (PPAP, GD&T).

🎤 Interview Format

Behavioral interview focused on teamwork and problem-solving. Technical questions related to electrical/mechanical engineering fundamentals. Questions about safety awareness and compliance. May include panel interview with multiple team members.

🏆 Success Stories

Duke Energy interns work on real utility projects and many receive return offers. Previous interns have worked on transmission line design, equipment reliability, and grid modernization. Strong candidates demonstrate both technical skills and understanding of the utility industry.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Application may ask: What interests you about the utility industry? Describe your experience with equipment analysis or reliability engineering. How do you approach safety in engineering projects?
