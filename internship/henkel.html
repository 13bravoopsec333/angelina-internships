📝 Description

Hands-on engineering internship at manufacturing facilities. Equipment maintenance, engineering projects, process optimization. Consumer brands and adhesive technologies.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled students, engineering majors

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Henkel's careers portal (henkel.com/careers). Search by location (US locations in CT, OH, GA) and career level (Students & Graduates). Henkel has two divisions relevant to materials: Adhesive Technologies (industrial adhesives, sealants) and Consumer Brands. Upload CV or import from LinkedIn. Henkel also recruits at career fairs — attend if Henkel comes to UF.

📄 Resume Tips

Henkel Adhesive Technologies directly uses polymer chemistry — highlight Sumerlin group research (chain polymerization, polymer characterization). NMR and FTIR are core analytical techniques for adhesive formulation. Emphasize CPP/PPAP experience from HNI for manufacturing quality. JMP for statistical analysis of formulation data. Show 'growth mindset' — Henkel explicitly looks for this. Quantify achievements (e.g., 'optimized X process reducing Y by Z%').

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Henkel's application process is structured: Online Application → Phone/Video Interview → In-Person Interview → Offer. Prepare for each stage. For the first interview, know Henkel's business — Adhesive Technologies is the world's largest adhesives company. Show genuine interest in how materials science drives adhesive innovation. Henkel values 'daring to make an impact' — demonstrate initiative. Build LinkedIn connections with Henkel recruiters and engineers.

🎤 Interview Format

Three-stage process: (1) Phone/video screen with recruiter (30 min) — career goals, fit assessment; (2) Phone/video with hiring manager (45-60 min) — technical skills, role fit; (3) In-person interview with HR and team — meet the team, discuss expectations. Tips from Henkel recruiters: prepare 3-5 personal/professional priorities, ask clarifying questions, be authentic, close with 'Is there any question I should be asking?'
