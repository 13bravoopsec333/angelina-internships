📝 Description

Internship and global graduate roles across business functions. Trusted brands company. Preparing students for careers in consumer products.

✅ Eligibility

Students and recent graduates

📋 Application Process

Applications open on scjohnson.com/careers or the SC Johnson Workday portal, typically in August–October for the following summer. SC Johnson is a family-owned consumer goods company (Windex, Raid, Ziploc, Glade, Scrubbing Bubbles) headquartered in Racine WI. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major match). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Hiring manager interview (45–60 min, virtual or on-site in Racine WI): behavioral STAR + technical discussion. (5) Offer within 2–4 weeks. SC Johnson offers both summer internships and early career rotational programs across Engineering, Supply Chain, Marketing, and R&D.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on scjohnson.com/careers (Workday portal). Also available on Handshake and LinkedIn. Apply within the first 2–3 weeks. SC Johnson is a family-owned company with ~13,000 employees — competition is moderate to high for their structured programs. UF is a viable target for engineering roles. Attend SC Johnson info sessions and career fairs for direct recruiter access. Referrals help.

Some positions may include a brief technical assessment or project discussion.
