✅ Eligibility

Students and recent graduates

📋 Application Process

Apply through SGS's careers portal. The process includes an online application with supplemental questions, followed by an initial phone screening, a technical assessment or online test, and then in-person or virtual interviews with team members. SGS operates 2,500+ labs across 115 countries, so opportunities are widely distributed. The structured process allows for thorough evaluation.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through SGS's official careers website (sgs.com/careers). Search for internship positions in the US — focus on materials testing, chemical analysis, or industrial inspection roles. Connect with SGS recruiters on LinkedIn. Attend any campus recruiting events. Mention your analytical skills (NMR, FTIR, JMP) — these are core to SGS's testing business. Employee referrals are effective.

📄 Resume Tips

Emphasize your analytical laboratory skills — NMR, FTIR, and JMP are directly relevant to SGS's testing and inspection work. Highlight your chemistry minor. Mention any quality management or ISO standards coursework. Include your materials characterization experience. Your manufacturing floor experience from HNI is relevant for industrial inspection. Mention your 3.96 GPA — SGS values technical competence.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply to multiple SGS locations — they have labs across the US. Mention your interest in testing, inspection, and certification. Highlight your laboratory skills and attention to detail. Research SGS's service areas (food testing, materials testing, environmental testing, etc.) and express specific interest. Your analytical background makes you a strong candidate. SGS values Swiss precision and quality — emphasize your meticulous approach.

🎤 Interview Format

Structured process with phone screening, technical assessment, and panel interviews. Expect questions about analytical methods, quality systems, and problem-solving. Behavioral questions about attention to detail and working in regulated environments. Technical questions about testing methodologies and materials analysis. Per Glassdoor, the process is thorough and well-organized.
