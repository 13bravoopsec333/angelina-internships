📝 Description

World leader in aluminum rolling and recycling. Science and engineering internships. Investing $2.5B in new Alabama facility. Global leader in sustainable aluminum — a niche within metals that's growing fast.

✅ Eligibility

Students in materials science, engineering, or related fields

📋 Application Process

Apply through novelis.com/careers or their Workday ATS. Complete online application with resume, transcript, and education details. Some roles require a cover letter or brief essay. Initial screening by campus recruiting team, followed by a phone screen with HR. Selected candidates do 2-3 rounds of interviews: a technical interview with the engineering team, a behavioral interview with the hiring manager, and potentially a site visit. Strong emphasis on understanding of materials science and manufacturing.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Directly through Novelis careers portal and through university career services. Novelis recruits at Materials Science and Engineering career fairs. They have strong partnerships with universities in the Southeast (Georgia Tech, UF, Auburn). Apply through both the portal and your university's career platform if available. Networking with Novelis engineers through professional societies (TMS, ASM) is effective.

📄 Resume Tips

This is a materials engineering dream role — lean heavily into your major coursework (metallurgy, polymers, materials characterization). Highlight any experience with materials testing, NMR, or analytical techniques from your Sumerlin research. Your Ansys/Creo/Solidworks skills are valuable for process modeling and part design. Mention familiarity with aluminum or metals if you have it, but polymer research is also valued since Novelis innovates in aluminum-polymer composites. Emphasize sustainability interest — aluminum recycling is core to Novelis's mission. One page, detail your lab skills prominently.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Novelis is the world's largest aluminum recycler — they want candidates passionate about sustainability and circular economy. Your materials engineering background is the exact fit. Your research experience (NMR, TLC, chain polymerization) demonstrates lab competency and analytical thinking. HNI internship shows manufacturing awareness. Frame everything through the lens of materials lifecycle — how understanding materials properties enables better recycling and manufacturing. Apply to Atlanta HQ for the broadest exposure, or a plant site for hands-on manufacturing experience. GPA of 3.5+ is competitive.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen (30 min, behavioral + GPA), followed by a 45-60 minute technical interview with 1-2 engineers or scientists. Technical questions cover materials fundamentals (phase diagrams, mechanical properties, corrosion), analytical methods (XRD, SEM, NMR), and problem-solving scenarios. Behavioral questions focus on teamwork, attention to detail, and interest in sustainability. Final round may include a plant tour or additional meeting with senior leadership.

🏆 Success Stories

Materials engineering students with lab research experience are the ideal candidates for Novelis internships. The company specifically recruits from materials science programs. Students who demonstrate genuine interest in aluminum recycling and sustainability, combined with strong lab skills, consistently get interviews. Interns who can connect their research experience to Novelis's recycling processes stand out. Many interns convert to full-time, especially in process engineering and R&D roles.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Some postings ask about specific areas of interest within aluminum recycling (rolling, casting, recycling technology, sustainability). They may ask about your understanding of aluminum alloys or metal recycling processes. Research Novelis's closed-loop recycling model and mention specific aspects that interest you. If asked about career goals, tie them to sustainable manufacturing.
