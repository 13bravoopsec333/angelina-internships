📝 Description

Global quality assurance provider. Materials testing and geotechnical engineering internships. Construction materials testing, environmental engineering, industrial hygiene, and specialty testing. A less obvious path for MSE students — quality/testing industry.

✅ Eligibility

Students in materials, civil, or geotechnical engineering

📋 Application Process

Apply via careers.intertek.com (Workday portal). Upload resume, complete short application with education/gpa/work authorization questions. Some positions ask for a brief cover letter. No separate internship portal — internships appear alongside full-time roles. Screening includes GPA verification (typically 3.0+), background check, and sometimes a short phone screen with the hiring manager or lab supervisor.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Directly through Intertek careers portal at careers.intertek.com. Search 'intern' or 'construction materials' and filter by location. Also attend career fairs — Intertek has a strong presence at materials/civil engineering career fairs especially in the Southeast and Northeast. LinkedIn can surface openings too, but the portal is primary.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight any hands-on testing lab experience, coursework in materials science, mechanics, or construction materials. Mention any CAD skills (Solidworks/Creo) and analytical tools (JMP). If you have lab safety training or ASTM/ISO standard familiarity, lead with that. Keep to one page. Quantify any testing volumes or precision metrics from your HNI internship. No need for a cover letter unless asked, but if one is requested, tie your materials engineering background to quality assurance and testing methodologies.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Intertek is a testing/inspection company so they value hands-on lab skills over GPA prestige. Your materials engineering background is a direct fit. Mention familiarity with standards (ASTM, ISO) if you have any from coursework. Your HNI internship with GD&T and quality processes (PPAP) is very relevant — testing interns support quality workflows. Apply to multiple locations simultaneously since different labs have separate budgets. Reach out to lab managers directly on LinkedIn after applying — smaller offices especially appreciate proactive candidates.

🎤 Interview Format

Typically 1-2 rounds: a phone screen with HR or the hiring manager (15-20 minutes, behavioral + basic technical questions about lab experience and coursework), followed by a potential in-person or video interview with the lab team. Questions focus on comfort with lab equipment, attention to detail, ability to follow procedures, and why testing/inspection interests you. Very rarely panel-style — usually 1-on-1.

🏆 Success Stories

Students with materials science or civil engineering backgrounds who have lab experience (even coursework labs) routinely get interviews. The bar is not extremely high — enthusiasm for testing/inspection work and basic lab safety knowledge are the main requirements. Many interns convert to full-time offers. A materials engineering major from a state school with 3.5+ GPA and lab coursework is a competitive applicant.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Some postings include 2-3 short supplemental questions: Why are you interested in testing/inspection? Describe a time you paid close attention to detail. What lab or technical experience do you have? Keep responses concise and specific — reference your materials engineering coursework or HNI experience.
