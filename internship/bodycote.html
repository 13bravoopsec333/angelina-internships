📝 Description

World's largest heat treatment and metallurgical services provider. 150+ facilities in 20+ countries. Specializes in aerospace, defense, automotive, energy, and medical heat treatment. A career path most MSE students never consider — commercial heat treatment is a massive industry.

✅ Eligibility

Students and recent graduates interested in metallurgy and heat treatment

📋 Application Process

Apply via bodycote.com/join-bodycote. Online application with resume. Apprentice programs currently live in UK and Germany (expanding to US). Internships/graduate roles posted on the careers page. Recruiter screen → hiring manager interview → site visit. Rolling hiring.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Bodycote's careers portal. For US positions, check bodycote.com/join-bodycote and LinkedIn for postings. Attend materials science career fairs. Network with Bodycote metallurgists through ASM International or TMS student chapters.

📄 Resume Tips

Emphasize metallurgy coursework and any heat treatment knowledge. Highlight characterization skills (NMR, FTIR are relevant to quality control in heat treatment). Show interest in metallurgical engineering specifically. Mention any failure analysis or materials testing experience. Include SolidWorks/Creo CAD skills.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Bodycote is the global leader in heat treatment—they want students passionate about metallurgy. Emphasize Sumerlin group polymer research (shows materials aptitude). Take any available metallurgy/materials science electives. Get ASM International student membership. Show willingness to work at manufacturing facilities (not just corporate). Understanding of aerospace/defense heat treatment standards is a plus.

🎤 Interview Format

Behavioral + technical interview. Expect questions on metallurgical fundamentals (phase diagrams, heat treatment processes, materials characterization). Site visit may include meeting the team and touring the facility. For apprenticeships, may include aptitude assessments.

🏆 Success Stories

Bodycote values hands-on problem solvers who want to work in manufacturing environments. Apprenticeship program is designed as a pipeline to full-time metallurgist/engineer roles. Strong performers get fast-tracked to senior technical roles. Interns who demonstrate quality mindset and attention to detail stand out.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Online application may ask: What interests you about heat treatment and metallurgy? Describe your experience with materials characterization. How do you approach quality control in manufacturing? Why Bodycote specifically?
