📝 Description

Spring 2027 applications due Sept 30, 2026. 10-week summer or 16-week fall/spring internships at DOE national labs. Materials science opportunities at Brookhaven, Argonne, Oak Ridge, NIST, etc. Apply via science.osti.gov.

✅ Eligibility

US citizens or permanent residents, enrolled undergrad students

📋 Application Process

1) Create account on ORAU portal (wdts.orau.gov) 2) Complete application with all required documents 3) Submit by deadline (Fall 2026 term: Sept 30, 2026 at 5pm ET; Spring 2027: TBD) 4) Application reviewed by program office 5) Matched with lab/mentor based on research interests 6) Notification ~8-10 weeks after deadline. Competitive — ~30% acceptance rate. Must be US citizen or permanent resident.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through the official ORAU portal (science.osti.gov/wdts/suli). Attend SULI Application Assistance Workshops (available via recorded presentation). Attend SULI Office Hours for Q&A. Apply to multiple terms if first attempt doesn't work. Express interest in specific DOE labs that align with materials science (e.g., NREL for energy materials, ORNL for materials science, Argonne for materials research).

📄 Resume Tips

Lead with Sumerlin Polymer Research Group experience — DOE labs value undergraduate research experience highly. Highlight specific techniques: NMR, FTIR, chain polymerization. Mention any computational skills (JMP, data analysis). Emphasize GPA (3.96 is excellent for SULI). Include relevant coursework in materials science, polymers, thermodynamics. One page, research-focused format.

Angelina's research experience with Sumerlin group makes her a very competitive SULI applicant. Her GPA of 3.96 is well above average. 🎤 Interview Format

SULI does not typically have a formal interview process. Selection is based on application materials: GPA, research experience, recommendation letters, personal statement, and research interest alignment with available mentors. Some labs may conduct a brief phone screen or video call before final selection, but most decisions are made from the application alone.

🏆 Success Stories

SULI is one of the most prestigious undergraduate research internships in the US. Many SULI alumni go on to top PhD programs and national lab careers. UF students regularly receive SULI awards. The internship provides genuine research experience working alongside PhD scientists and engineers. Housing and stipend make it financially accessible. Strong pipeline to DOE national lab employment.
