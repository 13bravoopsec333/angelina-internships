📝 Description

U.S. semiconductor foundry. Trusted supplier to DoD. Fab internship with hands-on semiconductor manufacturing experience. Florida location actively recruits UF students.

✅ Eligibility

Junior+ in STEM degree programs. U.S. Person required (ITAR).

📋 Application Process

Apply via jobs.dayforcehcm.com/en-US/skywater. Online application with resume. Recruiter screen → hiring manager interview → site tour. Apprenticeship program exists but not currently accepting applications. Rolling hiring for internships.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through SkyWater's job portal. Check for internship postings at university career fairs (especially at universities near Orlando or Minneapolis). LinkedIn networking with SkyWater engineers.

📄 Resume Tips

Emphasize semiconductor processing or materials science coursework. Highlight cleanroom experience if any. Mention any fabrication or process engineering experience. Include characterization skills (relevant to quality and yield). Show understanding of semiconductor manufacturing.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

SkyWater is a trusted foundry for defense, aerospace, and medical applications. Your materials background is directly relevant. Emphasize any process engineering or manufacturing experience. Show interest in the semiconductor industry's strategic importance. Orlando location is convenient for UF students. Highlight Ansys/FEA skills for process simulation roles.

🎤 Interview Format

Behavioral interview with hiring manager. Technical questions about semiconductor fabrication processes. May include a cleanroom tour. Questions about willingness to work in fab environment and safety awareness.

🏆 Success Stories

SkyWater interns gain hands-on experience in a real semiconductor fab environment. Previous interns have worked on process development, yield improvement, and equipment engineering. The company values students who are passionate about semiconductor manufacturing and willing to work in cleanroom environments.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Application may ask: What interests you about semiconductor manufacturing? Describe your experience with materials processing or fabrication. How do you approach quality and safety in a manufacturing environment?
