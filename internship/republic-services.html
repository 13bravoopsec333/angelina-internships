📝 Description

Second-largest US waste services company. Engineering and environmental internships at various FL operations. Check careers page for Florida postings.

✅ Eligibility

Engineering or environmental science students

📋 Application Process

1) Submit online application with resume and transcript. 2) Initial HR phone screen (20 min). 3) Hiring manager video interview (45 min). 4) On-site or virtual panel interview with team (1-2 hours). 5) Background check and drug test required. Offer within 2-3 weeks. Republic's process is similar to WM's but slightly faster for intern positions.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Republic Services' career portal — search for 'intern' or 'student' positions. Phoenix HQ and Arizona operations have the most openings. Republic recruits at Arizona State, University of Arizona, and other Southwest schools — check UF career fairs. LinkedIn connection with Republic engineers in environmental or operations roles can help. Mention sustainability interests in your application.

📄 Resume Tips

Republic values environmental engineering, civil engineering, and industrial engineering backgrounds. Your Materials Engineering degree is relevant for recycling technology, equipment durability, and materials testing. Highlight any sustainability coursework or projects. Your HNI manufacturing experience is relevant — Republic has manufacturing and maintenance operations. Mention any CAD skills (Creo, SolidWorks). Include GPA. Emphasize problem-solving and analytical skills.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Republic is the second-largest waste services company — show you understand their scale and environmental mission. Express interest in sustainability, recycling innovation, and renewable energy (Republic converts landfill gas to energy). Your polymer research experience is relevant for plastics recycling. Mention any process improvement or cost reduction projects from HNI. Republic values interns who are safety-conscious and environmentally aware. Apply early — Republic's intern cohort is smaller than WM's.

🎤 Interview Format

Two to three rounds: (1) HR phone screen with behavioral and logistics questions. (2) Technical video interview with hiring manager — questions about engineering fundamentals, environmental awareness, and problem-solving. (3) Final panel interview with team members. Republic asks about leadership experience, teamwork, and interest in environmental services. They value candidates who demonstrate initiative and a collaborative mindset. Less technical than WM's process.

🏆 Success Stories

Republic interns work on fleet optimization, recycling facility engineering, landfill management, and environmental compliance projects. The program includes rotations across different business units. Conversion rate is moderate to strong (50-60%). Past interns have become project engineers, environmental specialists, and operations managers. Republic emphasizes career development and promotes from within. The Phoenix location offers warm weather and outdoor activities.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Typical supplemental questions: 'Why Republic Services?', 'What experience do you have with environmental or sustainability initiatives?', 'Describe a technical project you're proud of.', 'How do you handle working in teams?', 'Are you comfortable working in industrial or outdoor environments?', 'What are your career goals in environmental engineering?' Republic may also ask about your interest in specific locations or business units.
