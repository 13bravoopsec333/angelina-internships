📝 Description

Internships in engineering, environmental science, IT, business, and more. TVA works with schools across the region. Engineering internships available in materials and infrastructure.

✅ Eligibility

US citizens, enrolled students

📋 Application Process

Apply through TVA careers portal (tva.com/careers). Submit resume, transcripts, and letters of recommendation. Followed by interviews. Positions are seasonal — summer is primary. TVA works with schools across the region (Southeast US). Hiring process takes average 54 days overall, 16 days for intern roles specifically.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through TVA careers portal when positions open (typically spring/summer for following year). TVA recruits heavily at Southeast universities — UF is a good fit geographically. For materials engineering, target engineering roles in power generation, grid reliability, or environmental management. Mention Ansys, Solidworks, materials characterization (FTIR, NMR). TVA's environmental group handles water quality, biodiversity, and sustainability — relevant for materials science.
