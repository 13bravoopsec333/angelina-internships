📝 Description

Fine ceramics and MEMS process development. Internships in Design, Process Engineering, and Test Engineering. Work in state-of-the-art cleanroom facilities. Unique opportunity in advanced ceramics and semiconductor materials. Global program with international trainees.

✅ Eligibility

Students in engineering, science, or related fields (Erasmus, international students welcome)

📋 Application Process

Apply through kyoceratechnologies.com/work-with-us/open-positions. Applications should be sent January–February for summer positions. The company offers internships in Design, Process Engineering, Test Engineering, and IT. After application review, there's typically a phone/video interview with the team lead. Thesis positions are often offered after a successful internship. International students from programs like Erasmus Mundus have been accepted.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly through Kyocera Technologies' open positions page. Email the company with your resume and a brief statement of interest — they accept open applications even when no positions are posted. Mention your MEMS or semiconductor interest if applicable. Connect with Kyocera engineers on LinkedIn. Note: Kyocera Technologies is the European (Finnish) subsidiary — different from Kyocera Corporation in Japan.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight your Ansys and SolidWorks skills — relevant for MEMS design and process engineering. Emphasize your materials engineering background — ceramics and semiconductor materials are core to Kyocera. Mention NMR/FTIR for materials characterization. Include any cleanroom or semiconductor fabrication experience. Your chemistry minor is valuable for process chemistry. Mention interest in MEMS technology if you have relevant coursework.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply January–February for summer positions — this is their preferred application window. Express interest in both internship and thesis tracks. Mention your willingness to work in cleanroom environments (Micronova). Research MEMS technology and Kyocera's product lines. Your materials engineering background is a strong fit. If you have any semiconductor or microfabrication coursework, highlight it prominently. International experience or language skills are a plus.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone or video interview with the hiring team. Technical questions about your engineering background and specific skills. Behavioral questions about teamwork and problem-solving. May include questions about your interest in MEMS or specific technologies. The Finnish work culture emphasizes flat hierarchies and collaborative problem-solving.

🏆 Success Stories

Kyocera Technologies has a strong track record of converting interns to full-time employees. Their website states: 'Some of the top-notch MEMS engineers at Kyocera Technologies have started out as trainees.' The company has hosted trainees from Erasmus Mundus Program and Tohoku University, Japan. Interns work in state-of-the-art Micronova cleanroom facilities.

📝 Supplemental Questions

No supplemental essays reported. Application requires resume and cover letter. You may be asked to describe your thesis interests or proposed project focus during the interview.
