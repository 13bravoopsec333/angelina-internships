📝 Description

One of the world's largest food companies. Engineering internships in manufacturing, R&D, supply chain. Check careers site for current openings.

✅ Eligibility

Engineering/food science students

📋 Application Process

Apply via jobs.kraftheinz.com/careers?query=student. Online application with resume. Recruiter screen → hiring manager interview → team meetings. 10-week immersive program with project presentation to local leadership. Rolling hiring.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Kraft Heinz's careers portal. Attend campus recruiting events at universities with strong engineering programs. Network through food science or engineering student organizations. Apply early for best consideration.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight manufacturing and process engineering experience. Emphasize GD&T, CAD, and quality systems skills (HNI PPAP experience is relevant). Include any food science or chemical engineering coursework. Show understanding of food manufacturing and packaging. Mention any process improvement or lean manufacturing experience.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Kraft Heinz values hands-on problem solvers who can deliver results. Your HNI internship with GD&T and quality systems is directly relevant. Show understanding of food manufacturing and supply chain. Emphasize ability to present to leadership (interns present their projects). Demonstrate quality mindset and attention to detail.

🎤 Interview Format

Behavioral interview focused on leadership and impact. Technical questions related to manufacturing engineering. Questions about presenting results and communicating with leadership. May include a case study or group exercise.

🏆 Success Stories

Kraft Heinz interns work on real projects with direct business impact. Previous interns have worked on process optimization, equipment improvement, and quality initiatives. Strong candidates receive full-time offers. The program is designed as a pipeline to leadership roles.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Application may ask: Describe a project where you delivered measurable results. How do you approach communicating technical results to leadership? What interests you about food manufacturing and the Kraft Heinz portfolio?
