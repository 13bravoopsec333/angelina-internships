📝 Description

Mixed-signal audio and voice IC. Codec, amplifier, and sensor IC design for mobile and consumer electronics.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled students in EE, CE, or related fields

📋 Application Process

Applications open on cirrus.com/careers (Workday ATS), typically in August–October for the following summer. Cirrus Logic is a fabless semiconductor company specializing in audio and voice signal processing — their chips are in Apple iPhones, Android devices, and laptops. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major, keywords — EE/CE/ChE preferred, Materials Eng considered for process/packaging roles). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Technical interview with hiring manager/team lead (45–60 min, virtual): behavioral STAR + technical depth discussion. (5) Offer within 2–4 weeks. Cirrus Logic is headquartered in Austin TX with ~2,000 employees. Intern roles span hardware engineering, firmware, software, and process engineering.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on cirrus.com/careers (Workday portal). Also available on LinkedIn. Apply within the first 2–3 weeks. Cirrus Logic is a niche semiconductor company — they recruit at specific universities with strong EE/CE/Materials programs. UF is a viable target. Attend Cirrus Logic info sessions and career fairs for direct recruiter access. The company has a strong internship-to-full-time pipeline.

For process engineering roles, emphasize any semiconductor fabrication or cleanroom experience. Cirrus Logic values technical depth — be prepared to discuss specific projects in detail. Note: Cirrus Logic primarily hires EE/CE majors, but Materials Engineering students with semiconductor interest can apply for process/packaging roles.
