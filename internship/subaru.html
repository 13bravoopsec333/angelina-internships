📝 Description

Immersive internships in advanced automotive manufacturing. Project management and hands-on experience. AIM Program for manufacturing.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled students. 2027 opportunities coming soon.

📋 Application Process

Apply through hcal.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience (Subaru's Oracle ATS). Complete detailed online application including resume, transcript, education history, and work authorization. Some positions require a cover letter or essay. Initial screening by HR, then a phone interview with the hiring manager. Final round may be a virtual panel interview with engineering team members and potentially a plant tour. Strong emphasis on GPA (3.0+ minimum, 3.5+ preferred) and engineering fundamentals.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through Subaru's Oracle careers portal, searching for 'intern' and filtering by Lafayette IN or other locations. Subaru recruits at SAE and mechanical engineering career fairs. They have strong partnerships with Purdue (near Lafayette) and other Midwest engineering schools. Apply through university career services if they have a partnership with your school. Networking at automotive industry events is valuable.

📄 Resume Tips

For automotive engineering, highlight any vehicle-related projects (Formula SAE, automotive clubs, car repair experience). Your materials engineering background is relevant for materials testing, quality, and supplier engineering roles. Emphasize CAD skills (Solidworks, Creo) and GD&T knowledge from HNI — critical for automotive engineering. Mention any manufacturing or production experience. Your PPAP knowledge from HNI is directly applicable — Subaru uses PPAP extensively with suppliers. One page, professional formatting.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Subaru values quality, safety, and continuous improvement (Kaizen) — your HNI internship with PPAP and GD&T demonstrates understanding of these automotive quality concepts. Your materials engineering background is relevant for materials engineering and supplier quality roles. Mention interest in Subaru's manufacturing processes and their Lafayette plant specifically. The plant is in a college town atmosphere — show you're comfortable in that environment. Apply early — Subaru's intern positions fill quickly due to limited openings. GPA of 3.5+ and relevant project experience make you competitive.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with HR (20-30 min, behavioral, GPA, citizenship status), followed by a technical interview with the hiring manager or team lead (30-45 min). Questions focus on engineering fundamentals, problem-solving approach, and understanding of manufacturing/quality concepts. Behavioral questions emphasize teamwork, attention to detail, and commitment to quality. Final round may be a panel interview with multiple engineers and a plant tour (virtual or in-person).

🏆 Success Stories

Engineering students with manufacturing or quality experience are strong candidates. Subaru specifically values candidates who understand automotive quality systems (PPAP, FMEA, SPC). Students from Midwest engineering schools (Purdue, IU, Rose-Hulman) have strong placement rates, but Southeastern students with automotive experience also get interviews. The intern program is structured as a pipeline to full-time — most interns who perform well receive return offers.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Some postings ask about automotive experience, specific engineering interests (manufacturing, quality, design), and why Subaru specifically. Research Subaru's manufacturing philosophy (lean manufacturing, Kaizen, quality-first culture) and mention specific aspects that resonate with you. If you have any automotive project experience, describe it in detail.
