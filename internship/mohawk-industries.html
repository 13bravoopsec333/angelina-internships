📝 Description

Largest flooring manufacturer globally with FL distribution and manufacturing operations. Engineering internships in manufacturing, process engineering, quality. Materials science relevant for flooring materials.

✅ Eligibility

Students in engineering, manufacturing, materials science

📋 Application Process

Apply through Mohawk careers portal (careers.mohawkind.com). College recruiting page indicates interns are 'presented a real business challenge within their functional area' and 'come together to showcase their accomplishments' at the end. Standard application: resume + cover letter. Mohawk recruits heavily from southeast universities for their Dalton, GA headquarters. The program includes a capstone-style presentation to leadership.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through careers.mohawkind.com and search for 'intern' + 'Dalton, GA'. Check UF Handshake — Mohawk recruits at southeast universities. Attend UF engineering career fairs where Mohawk has a presence. For Dalton, GA location specifically, UF and Georgia Tech are target schools. LinkedIn networking with Mohawk engineers in Dalton is effective. Also check LinkedIn for specific internship postings.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: manufacturing process knowledge, Creo/Solidworks CAD, Ansys Granta materials selection (polymer selection directly relevant to flooring), JMP statistical analysis, quality systems (PPAP from HNI), GD&T. Mohawk makes flooring products (carpet, tile, laminate) — emphasize any polymer/materials processing coursework. Your polymer research in Sumerlin group is highly relevant to Mohawk's material science needs. Mention lean manufacturing or continuous improvement knowledge if any. Production and process engineering skills are valued.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) Research Mohawk's product lines — carpet tiles, luxury vinyl, ceramic tile, laminate. Understanding their materials is key. 2) Dalton, GA is a small town — emphasize willingness to relocate. 3) The capstone presentation at program end means they value communication skills — mention any presentation experience. 4) Mohawk values continuous improvement — mention any lean/six sigma exposure. 5) UF Materials Advantage connections may have Mohawk contacts. 6) Apply early — Mohawk hires summer interns in fall.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screening with HR, then video or in-person interview with hiring manager and team. Behavioral questions focused on teamwork, problem-solving, and manufacturing interest. May include a brief technical assessment. Questions about your understanding of manufacturing processes and quality systems. Less technical than aerospace companies — more about general engineering aptitude and cultural fit.

🏆 Success Stories

Mohawk's internship program is structured like a consulting project — interns work on a real business challenge and present solutions to leadership. LinkedIn posts from former Mohawk interns highlight the hands-on manufacturing exposure and mentorship. Students from Georgia Tech and other southeast universities are common. The program is described as giving interns genuine ownership of projects. Many interns convert to full-time offers.
