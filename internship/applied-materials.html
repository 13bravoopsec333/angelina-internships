📝 Description

Summer, co-op and yearlong opportunities. Semiconductor equipment manufacturing. Engineering, science and business roles.

✅ Eligibility

Undergraduate and graduate students in engineering, science, business

📋 Application Process

Apply through Applied Materials careers portal or LinkedIn. Standard application: resume + cover letter. Applied Materials is the world's largest semiconductor equipment manufacturer — they make the machines that make chips. Internships span engineering, materials science, manufacturing, and software. The application is relatively straightforward with no supplemental essays.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through careers.appliedmaterials.com and LinkedIn. Check UF Handshake. Applied Materials recruits at top engineering universities including UF. Attend their information sessions at UF if available. LinkedIn networking is very effective — connect with Applied Materials engineers and recruiters. For Santa Clara CA location, also check Stanford/Berkeley career fairs for timing. For Austin TX, check UT Austin events. Cold-email recruiters on LinkedIn with your resume attached.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Science/Engineering coursework, GPA 3.96, Ansys Granta (materials selection — directly relevant), Creo/Solidworks CAD, FTIR and materials characterization (Applied Materials does semiconductor materials analysis), NMR spectroscopy. Mention any experience with thin films, deposition, etching, or semiconductor processes if applicable. Emphasize JMP statistical analysis (process optimization is critical in semiconductor manufacturing). HNI internship PPAP experience shows quality systems knowledge. The Sumerlin polymer research group demonstrates research capability.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) Research Applied Materials' product lines: etch systems, CVD/PVD, ion implantation, CMP, inspection. Understanding what they do is crucial for interviews. 2) Semiconductor industry knowledge is a huge plus — read up on Moore's Law, advanced packaging, heterogeneous integration. 3) Santa Clara CA and Austin TX are competitive locations — mention flexibility on location. 4) Apply early — semiconductor companies recruit 6+ months ahead. 5) Network with UF alumni at Applied Materials on LinkedIn. 6) If you have any cleanroom experience, highlight it prominently. 7) Applied Materials values innovation — mention any patents, publications, or creative solutions from your research.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screening with recruiter, then 2-3 rounds of technical interviews with engineers/managers. Technical questions about materials science fundamentals, semiconductor processes, and problem-solving scenarios. Behavioral questions about teamwork and project management. May include a brief technical assessment or case study. The interviews are technical but not overly difficult for a materials engineering student.

🏆 Success Stories

Glassdoor reviews for Applied Materials internships describe a structured program with mentorship, project presentations, and networking events. Engineering interns work on real semiconductor equipment projects. Successful candidates typically have strong GPA, relevant materials science coursework, and demonstrated interest in semiconductor technology. Reddit users on r/ChemicalEngineering and r/materialsScience note that Applied Materials values research experience and analytical skills. Interns frequently convert to full-time offers.
