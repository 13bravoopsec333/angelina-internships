📝 Description

Precious metals, healthcare, semiconductor & electronics, industrial materials.

✅ Eligibility

Students & Early Career

📋 Application Process

Apply through jobs.heraeus.com → Create profile → Submit application → Recruiter screening → Interview(s) → Offer. Positions posted on rolling basis. Some roles require degree alignment (materials science, chemical engineering preferred).

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Directly via jobs.heraeus.com portal. Filter by 'Early Professional' or 'Internship' categories. Also check Handshake for Heraeus postings targeting university students.

📄 Resume Tips

Emphasize materials science coursework, analytical skills, and lab experience. Heraeus works with precious metals and advanced materials — highlight any characterization experience (NMR, FTIR you already have is excellent). Mention GPA. German language skills are a plus given HQ in Hanau.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply to multiple Heraeus locations. Their US operations are growing, especially semiconductor materials in Dayton OH and advanced materials in Buford GA. Reference specific Heraeus business units (Heraeus Electronics, Heraeus Sedanalytics) in cover letter. Networking through LinkedIn with Heraeus materials scientists.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with HR → Virtual technical interview with hiring manager → On-site or virtual panel. Behavioral questions with technical depth. May include a brief technical presentation or case study.

🏆 Success Stories

Heraeus promotes from within and values diverse backgrounds. They emphasize meaningful, sustainable work and employee development. Interns and thesis students often transition to full-time roles in R&D or manufacturing.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Expect: Why Heraeus? How does your materials background prepare you for work with precious metals/semiconductor materials? Describe a challenging analytical project you've worked on.
