📝 Description

Engineering/R&D internships and co-ops. Product, material, process, mechanical, electrical, and packaging development. Multidisciplinary teams with experienced engineers.

✅ Eligibility

Engineering students (mechanical, chemical, electrical, packaging)

📋 Application Process

Apply through kimberly-clark.com/careers or their ATS portal. Complete detailed online application with resume, transcript, and education history. Kimberly-Clark has a structured campus recruiting process with specific university partnerships. Initial screening by HR, then a phone screen with the hiring manager. Selected candidates do a 30-45 minute video interview with the hiring manager and potentially a team member. Strong emphasis on GPA (3.0+ minimum) and engineering fundamentals.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through Kimberly-Clark's careers portal and through university career services. Kimberly-Clark recruits at chemical engineering and materials science career fairs. They have strong partnerships with universities in the Southeast and Midwest. Attend their information sessions and campus events — they often extend interview invitations on the spot. Networking with Kimberly-Clark engineers through professional societies is effective. Apply through Handshake if available.

📄 Resume Tips

For consumer products engineering, emphasize coursework in process engineering, materials science, and quality systems. Your materials engineering background is relevant for materials selection, packaging, and product development. Highlight any manufacturing experience from HNI (PPAP, GD&T, quality processes are directly transferable). Mention CAD skills (Solidworks, Creo) for equipment and product design. JMP for statistical process control. Your research experience (NMR, TLC) demonstrates analytical skills for materials characterization. One page, professional formatting.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Kimberly-Clark values candidates who understand manufacturing processes and consumer products — your HNI internship is very relevant. Your materials engineering background is applicable to materials development and process engineering roles. The co-op program offers multiple rotations, so express interest in rotating across different functions. Apply to multiple locations — manufacturing plants offer more hands-on experience. Show genuine interest in consumer products and how materials science applies to tissue, hygiene, and packaging products. GPA of 3.0+ is sufficient — they weight manufacturing interest and practical experience highly.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with HR (20 min, behavioral + GPA), followed by a 30-45 minute video interview with the hiring manager. Questions focus on engineering fundamentals, manufacturing interest, and teamwork. Behavioral questions emphasize problem-solving, attention to detail, and working in production environments. Final round may be a plant visit or panel interview with engineering team members. The tone is professional but conversational.

🏆 Success Stories

Engineering students with manufacturing or consumer products experience are strong candidates. Kimberly-Clark specifically values candidates who understand production environments and quality systems. Students from state schools with good GPAs and relevant project experience are very competitive. The co-op program is designed as a pipeline to full-time engineering roles. Many co-ops convert to full-time, especially those who demonstrate technical competence and fit with the company culture.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Some postings ask about manufacturing experience, specific engineering interests (process, quality, packaging, maintenance), and why Kimberly-Clark specifically. Research Kimberly-Clark's brands (Kleenex, Huggies, Scott) and manufacturing operations. Mention specific aspects of consumer products manufacturing that interest you — product development, process optimization, quality assurance. If you have any consumer products experience, describe it.
