📝 Description

10-week summer or 16-week semester research at Brookhaven. Materials science, quantum materials, and energy research opportunities.

✅ Eligibility

Full-time student, 3.0+ GPA, US citizen/permanent resident, 18+, health insurance required

📋 Application Process

Apply through internships.energy.gov (DOE centralized portal) — same portal as PNNL SULI. Requires: transcript, resume, statement of interest, faculty recommendation letter. Brookhaven's SULI program is one of the most prestigious DOE internships. Two application cycles per year. After submission, Brookhaven mentors review applications and select based on research fit. Phone/video interviews conducted by mentor PIs. Brookhaven has strong programs in materials science, physics, chemistry, and synchrotron science.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through internships.energy.gov for SULI. Also check Brookhaven's own careers portal (www.bnl.gov) for non-SULI internship postings. Brookhaven has the National Synchrotron Light Source (NSLS-II) — a world-class materials characterization facility. For materials science: apply to groups working at NSLS-II or the Chemistry Department. UF materials faculty may have Brookhaven collaborators — ask for introductions. Email potential mentors directly with your resume and research interests.

📄 Resume Tips

Lead with: Materials Science/Engineering major, GPA 3.96, polymer research experience (Sumerlin group), NMR and TLC analytical techniques, chain polymerization knowledge, FTIR spectroscopy, Ansys Granta materials selection. Brookhaven's synchrotron (NSLS-II) is ideal for materials characterization — mention any experience with X-ray techniques, spectroscopy, or structural analysis. Highlight research methodology skills from Sumerlin group. Include any publications or conference presentations. Brookhaven values collaborative research — mention team-based research experience.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) Brookhaven's NSLS-II is a major draw — mention interest in synchrotron-based materials research. 2) Email potential mentors in the Chemistry or Materials Science departments before applying. 3) Apply to BOTH SULI (DOE portal) AND Brookhaven's direct postings. 4) Your statement of interest should reference specific Brookhaven research or facilities. 5) UF's Materials Science program may have Brookhaven connections — check with faculty. 6) Brookhaven is on Long Island, NY — mention willingness to relocate. 7) Apply early in the application window. 8) The mentor's recommendation is crucial — build rapport during the interview.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone or video interview (20-30 min) with the potential mentor/PI. Questions focus on: research interests, specific techniques you want to learn, how your background prepares you, career goals. May include questions about Brookhaven's specific facilities or research programs. The mentor has significant say in selection — building rapport and demonstrating genuine interest in their work is key. Technical questions about your prior research experience are common.

🏆 Success Stories

Brookhaven SULI is one of the most competitive DOE internships. Reddit users on r/Physics and r/ChemicalEngineering describe successful applications by demonstrating interest in specific Brookhaven facilities (NSLS-II, CFN). Students with strong analytical skills and research experience are preferred. Successful applicants typically have 3.5+ GPA and prior research experience. Brookhaven mentors value students who can work independently and have strong analytical backgrounds. Former interns describe the experience as transformative for their research careers.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Statement of interest explaining: your research interests, why you want to work at Brookhaven specifically, what skills you bring, how the experience fits your career goals. Faculty recommendation letter is required. Unofficial transcript.
