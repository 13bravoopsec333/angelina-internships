📝 Description

Engineering co-ops and internships in power generation (nuclear, fossil), transmission, distribution, and technology. 3 alternating semesters for co-ops. Locations in GA, AL, MS, IL, VA.

✅ Eligibility

GPA 2.8+ (3.0 for internships), enrolled in engineering or related program

📋 Application Process

1) Apply at southerncompany.jobs (search by company: Georgia Power or Southern Company). 2) Create Candidate Profile. 3) Recruiter review. 4) Phone screening. 5) Technical/behavioral interview. 6) Offer. Co-ops are 3 alternating rotations (spring/summer/fall); internships are single semester. Fall, Spring, and Summer positions available.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply at southerncompany-campus.jobs and create a Candidate Profile. Attend university career fairs — Southern Company recruits heavily at target schools (Georgia Tech, Auburn, Alabama, UAB, Kennesaw State, and many HBCUs). Engineering co-ops are direct pipelines to full-time careers. Atlanta GA is primary location for Georgia Power. Check their campus recruiting calendar for events at your university.
