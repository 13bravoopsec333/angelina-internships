📝 Description

Paid mentored research across biology, energy science, fusion/fission, national security, neutron science, physical sciences. 10-week summer or 16-week semester terms.

✅ Eligibility

Undergrad/grad student or recent graduate, 3.0+ GPA, US citizen or permanent resident, 18+

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Three pathways: (1) DOE Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internship (SULI) — apply through DOE Office of Science (science.energy.gov) — program covers ORNL and all DOE labs; (2) Direct ORNL internships through ORNL careers portal; (3) USAJOBS.gov for federal positions. SULI is the primary pathway for undergrads at ORNL. Application deadlines: SULI summer applications typically due January. ORNL also has the Mickey Leland Energy Fellowship (MLEF) and GEM fellowship pathways.

📄 Resume Tips

ORNL is a materials science powerhouse — emphasize your strongest technical skills. NMR, FTIR from Sumerlin group are core ORNL analytical techniques. Ansys for simulation, Creo/Solidworks for equipment design. Highlight any coursework in neutron science, diffraction, or advanced characterization (ORNL has the Spallation Neutron Source). Your 3.96 GPA is very competitive. Windchill for data management is a plus for ORNL's large-scale projects.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

ORNL is in Oak Ridge, TN — apply to SULI program (covers housing + stipend). Target materials science divisions: Materials Science and Technology Division, Neutron Scattering Division. Contact ORNL researchers whose work aligns with your interests — many SULI positions are created by direct researcher outreach. Apply to SULI by the January deadline. Your polymer research background is valued for ORNL's energy storage and advanced manufacturing programs.

🎤 Interview Format

SULI application is competitive but doesn't always require an interview — selection is based on application materials and researcher match. Direct ORNL positions may include phone screen + technical video interview. Focus on research interests, technical skills, and how your background aligns with ORNL's mission areas.

🏆 Success Stories

SULI interns at ORNL work alongside world-class scientists on cutting-edge research. Many go on to graduate programs at top universities or permanent positions at DOE labs. ORNL's materials characterization facilities are among the best in the world — interns gain hands-on experience with unique instruments.
