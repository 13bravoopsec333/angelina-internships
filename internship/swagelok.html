📝 Description

Specialty fluid system components manufacturer. Summer interns evaluate and test materials for valve, fitting, and connector systems. Niche in fluid systems and specialty metals — a family-owned company with a strong engineering culture.

✅ Eligibility

Students in engineering or related fields

📋 Application Process

1. Apply at swagelok.com/careers -> Careers. 2. Upload resume, complete brief application (GPA, location preference, work authorization). 3. Recruiter phone screen (15-20 min). 4. Virtual interview with hiring manager (30-45 min). 5. On-site interview at Solon facility (45-60 min with team, includes facility tour). 6. Some roles include a technical assessment or engineering challenge. 7. Offer within 1-2 weeks.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on swagelok.com/careers. Swagelok recruits at Ohio and Midwest schools (Case Western, Ohio State, University of Akron, Kent State, Cleveland State). Attend campus career fairs in the Cleveland area. Swagelok is a precision fluid systems company -- emphasize mechanical engineering and manufacturing background. Connect with Swagelok engineers on LinkedIn.

📄 Resume Tips

GPA 3.0+. Swagelok makes valves, fittings, and fluid system components -- highlight mechanical design, GD&T, materials science, and any experience with precision manufacturing. Your Creo/SolidWorks and GD&T experience from HNI is directly applicable. Emphasize any experience with machining, assembly, testing, or quality control. Include any experience with stainless steel, alloys, or materials selection. Show understanding of fluid systems or pressure testing.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Swagelok is a privately held company with a strong engineering culture -- show genuine interest in precision engineering and manufacturing. Research their product lines (valves, fittings, regulators, fluid system solutions). Apply to Solon, OH HQ for best access to engineering roles. Follow up with thank-you emails. Swagelok values quality and reliability -- mention any experience with quality systems or continuous improvement. Show willingness to work in manufacturing environments.

🎤 Interview Format

2 rounds typical. Round 1: Recruiter phone screen (behavioral + GPA). Round 2: Hiring manager + engineering team interview. Technical questions about mechanical design, materials, manufacturing processes. May include a facility tour or shop floor visit. Behavioral questions about problem-solving, attention to detail, and teamwork. Dress is business casual.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Common: 'Why Swagelok?' (mention precision engineering, quality leadership, fluid system innovation). 'Describe your experience with mechanical design or precision manufacturing.' 'What do you know about materials selection for fluid systems?' 'How do you approach quality control in manufacturing?' 'Tell me about a time you worked on a technically challenging project.' 'What experience do you have with GD&T or tolerance analysis?'
