📝 Description

Source Career Field internship at Patrick Space Force Base. Geospatial intelligence and defense. Full-time position.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled in college/university, US citizenship required, security clearance

📋 Application Process

Apply through NGA career portal (careers.nga.mil). Application period opens ~August for following summer (runs through end of September). Requires US citizenship, 18+ years old, enrolled at least half-time in accredited college, able to obtain TS/SCI clearance. Must pass drug screening and counterintelligence polygraph. Process takes 2 months to over a year due to security clearance. Application window is narrow — one month only.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply the moment the posting opens (~August). Security clearance processing takes months — early application is critical. Tailor resume to NGA's GEOINT mission: spatial analysis, data visualization, remote sensing. Mention any GIS/mapping coursework. Angelina's materials engineering background is less directly relevant but analytical skills (NMR, FTIR, JMP) transfer well. Emphasize citizenship and willingness to undergo clearance.

📄 Resume Tips

NGA values analytical thinking and technical skills. Highlight JMP statistical analysis experience, data processing skills, and analytical instrumentation (NMR, FTIR). Mention any spatial/geospatial coursework or projects. Emphasize ability to work with complex datasets. GPA 3.96 is excellent. NGA accepts a wide variety of degree fields — materials engineering qualifies. Do NOT list classified information on resume.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply immediately when posting opens — the clearance process alone takes months. NGA hires across many disciplines, not just GIS. Your analytical/instrumentation background is valuable. Be prepared for extensive background investigation. The polygraph is mandatory. NGA offers return opportunities — successful interns can convert to full-time after graduation (480 internship hours + good evaluation). Security clearance is extremely valuable for career even if you don't stay at NGA.

🎤 Interview Format

Structured panel interview with pre-selected questions. Formal and transactional. Questions focus on analytical skills, problem-solving, and mission alignment. May include technical questions about data analysis or geospatial concepts. Security clearance interview is separate and extensive.

🏆 Success Stories

Glassdoor: Interviews are structured and formal, panel format with pre-selected questions. Some candidates found process 'transactional' rather than conversational. Interns are guaranteed mission-relevant work. NGA provides tours of Intelligence Community agencies, networking events, luncheons. Benefits include paid leave, flexible schedule, onsite fitness facility. Return offers available after successful evaluation.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Application through NGA portal requires detailed personal information for security clearance processing. May need to disclose foreign contacts, travel history, financial records. Statement of interest in GEOINT mission may be required.
