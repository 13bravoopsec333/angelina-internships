🚀 Best Way to Apply

Email your resume directly to HR/careers at McDanel Materials. Also submit through their Paycor careers portal. For a small company like McDanel, a direct email with a strong cover letter is often more effective than online portals.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight materials science/engineering coursework, especially ceramics, composites, or advanced materials. Mention Ansys/CAE skills. GD&T knowledge from HNI is relevant. Emphasize quality systems experience. Small company — they value versatile, hands-on people.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

McDanel is a smaller company (Pittsburgh area) — personal outreach goes a long way. Find the hiring manager on LinkedIn and send a brief, professional message with your resume. Research their markets: aerospace, defense, semiconductor, medical/pharma. Show genuine interest in technical ceramics.

Likely phone screening → On-site interview with team. More informal given smaller company size. Technical discussion + culture fit. May include plant tour.

🏆 Success Stories

McDanel emphasizes passion, accountability, commitment to excellence, and service orientation (PACE values). Employees report meaningful impact and visibility across the organization. Small team means interns get significant responsibility.
