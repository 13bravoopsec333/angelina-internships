📝 Description

8-12 week summer internships. Transportation, infrastructure, environmental engineering. Real-world experience working alongside world-class professionals.

✅ Eligibility

Enrolled in at least sophomore year. Pursuing BS in Civil Engineering or related. Min GPA 3.0. US work authorization.

📋 Application Process

Applications are accepted on jacobs.com/careers (Workday ATS) on a rolling basis, typically opening in August–September for the following summer. Process: (1) Apply online with resume — no cover letter required. (2) ATS screening filters by GPA (3.0 minimum), major, and keyword matching. (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min): logistics, qualifications, behavioral fit. (4) Hiring manager interview (45–60 min, virtual or on-site): mix of behavioral STAR questions and light technical discussion based on your resume/projects. (5) Offer extended within 2–4 weeks of final interview. Some positions may include a brief technical assessment or case discussion. No coding tests for most engineering roles. Jacobs hires interns across water, environment, buildings, advanced manufacturing, energy, and transportation — positions are posted by individual business unit.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on jacobs.com/careers (Workday portal). Filter by 'Student/Graduate' and location. Also available through Handshake and university career fairs. Apply within the first 2–3 weeks of posting — rolling review means early applicants get the most open positions. UF has a strong Jacobs recruiting pipeline. Get a referral from a current Jacobs employee if possible — it significantly expedites screening.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA (well above their 3.0 floor), Creo/Solidworks CAD, Ansys Granta (materials selection — relevant to consulting), GD&T from HNI (precision manufacturing), PPAP (quality systems), NMR/FTIR (analytical instrumentation). Jacobs values consulting-adjacent skills: communication, project management, cross-functional teamwork. Keywords: materials characterization, quality assurance, manufacturing processes, CAD/FEA, environmental compliance, sustainable materials. Tailor to the specific business unit (water/environment vs. buildings vs. advanced manufacturing).

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 2–3 weeks of the posting window. Target specific business units that align with your Materials Engineering background (Advanced Manufacturing, Environmental, Water). Attend UF career fairs and Jacobs info sessions for direct recruiter access. Prepare STAR stories emphasizing consulting-relevant skills: problem-solving, client communication, cross-functional teamwork, and process improvement. Jacobs values candidates who can explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders. The 3.0 GPA is a hard gate — your 3.96 is well above it. Return-offer rate is estimated at 60–70% for strong performers.

🎤 Interview Format

Typically 1–2 rounds. Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min) followed by a virtual or on-site interview with the hiring manager and/or team lead (45–60 min). Mix of behavioral STAR questions ('Tell me about a time you worked on a team project') and light technical questions based on your resume. Glassdoor rates interview difficulty 2.5–3.0/5. No panel grilling, no coding tests for most engineering roles. Some consulting-focused positions may include a brief case-style question about approaching a problem.

📝 Supplemental Questions

No supplemental essays or cover letter required. The online application may include screening questions about GPA, work authorization, location preference, and willingness to relocate. Some positions ask about specific technical skills or certifications.
