📝 Description

Summer internships across engineering disciplines. Mentorship program, real project work, professional development. Co-op options available.

✅ Eligibility

Pursuing BS or MS in ME, EE, CE, or related from accredited program. Min GPA 3.0. US work authorization.

📋 Application Process

Apply through burnsmcd.com/careers/find-your-career/internships or their Workday ATS. Complete online application with resume, cover letter (optional but recommended), and education details. Burns & McDonnell is an employee-owned company — they emphasize culture fit heavily. Initial screening by HR, followed by a phone screen with a recruiter. Selected candidates do a 30-minute video interview with the hiring manager and potentially a team member. The process is relatively quick — decisions often made within 2-3 weeks of applying.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Through the Burns & McDonnell careers portal. They attend career fairs at hundreds of universities annually — one of the most active recruiters nationally. Handshake and university career services are key channels. They also host information sessions and office visits. Employee-owners often refer candidates — networking through alumni or professional connections is very effective. Apply early in the fall cycle — positions fill on a rolling basis.

📄 Resume Tips

Tailor to engineering consulting — emphasize problem-solving, teamwork, and communication skills alongside technical abilities. Your materials engineering background is relevant for materials consulting, environmental science, and construction materials roles. Mention CAD skills (Solidworks, Creo) and any project management or client interaction experience from HNI. Burns & McDonnell values well-rounded candidates — include leadership roles, community involvement, and extracurriculars. One page, clean professional formatting.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Burns & McDonnell is employee-owned and intensely values culture and teamwork. They want candidates who are enthusiastic about consulting, can communicate technical concepts clearly, and will thrive in a collaborative environment. Your HNI internship experience is valuable — it shows you can work in a professional engineering environment. Mention any client-facing or cross-functional experience. Apply to the Kansas City HQ for the broadest exposure, or a regional office for more focused work. GPA of 3.0+ is sufficient — they weight culture fit and enthusiasm highly. Attend their information sessions and career fair booths — showing genuine interest matters.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone screen with recruiter (15-20 min, behavioral, GPA, location preference), followed by a 30-minute video interview with the hiring manager. Questions focus on technical interest, teamwork, problem-solving, and communication skills. Behavioral questions use the STAR format. They may ask about specific projects or coursework that demonstrate your engineering abilities. Very rarely panel-style — usually 1-on-1. The tone is conversational and friendly.

🏆 Success Stories

Burns & McDonnell hires interns from a wide range of engineering disciplines and universities. They specifically value candidates who demonstrate strong communication skills and genuine enthusiasm for the work. Students from state schools with good GPAs and demonstrated leadership are very competitive. The employee-ownership culture means they invest heavily in intern development. Many interns convert to full-time — the company promotes from within aggressively.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Burns & McDonnell typically does not use supplemental questions in the initial application, but some roles may include a brief essay about why you're interested in engineering consulting. If asked, emphasize your desire to work on diverse projects, solve real-world problems, and grow technically in a collaborative environment. Research their key practice areas (power, water, construction, aviation) and mention one that aligns with your interests.
