📝 Description

12-week internships and 6-month co-ops in engineering, R&D, supply chain, finance. Mentorship from managers, coaches, and executive sponsors.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled students, engineering/food science majors

📋 Application Process

1. Apply online at campbells.com/careers -> Students & Interns tab. 2. Upload resume, complete short application (GPA, work authorization, location preference). 3. Some roles require a cover letter or short-answer questions about food interest. 4. Recruiter phone screen (15-20 min, behavioral + GPA verification). 5. Virtual or on-site interview with hiring manager + 1-2 team members (45-60 min each). 6. Final interview may include a plant tour or panel. 7. Offer within 1-2 weeks of final round.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on campbells.com/careers. Campbell's also recruits at Rutgers, Penn State, Drexel, and select NFPA/society events. Attend their info sessions at target schools. Connect with Campbell's campus recruiters on LinkedIn. Mention specific brand passion (e.g., Snyder's, Pace, Rao's) in your application.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight any food science, packaging, or consumer products coursework. If no food experience, emphasize transferable lab skills (FTIR, materials testing, statistical analysis with JMP). Campbell's values Lean/Six Sigma exposure. List GPA prominently (they screen at 3.0+). Include any GD&T, CAD (SolidWorks/Creo), or process engineering experience from your HNI internship. Show genuine interest in the food industry.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Attend Campbell's campus recruiting events and request informational interviews with alumni at the company. Express passion for the food/consumer products industry in every touchpoint. If you have polymer or packaging materials background, highlight it -- Campbell's invests heavily in sustainable packaging. Follow up within 24 hours of any interview with a personalized thank-you email. Apply to multiple roles (R&D, packaging engineering, quality) to increase odds.

🎤 Interview Format

Typically 2 rounds: (1) Recruiter phone/video screen (behavioral: tell me about yourself, why Campbell's, career goals). (2) Virtual or on-site panel interview with hiring manager + cross-functional team. Behavioral + technical questions. May include a case study or presentation on a food/packaging challenge. Dress is business casual. Expect questions like 'Tell me about a time you worked on a cross-functional team' and technical questions about food processing or packaging materials.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Typical supplemental: 'Why are you interested in Campbell's?' (reference specific brands, innovation in sustainable packaging, their Purpose-driven culture). 'Describe a time you solved a problem using data.' 'What experience do you have with food products or consumer goods?' 'How do you handle working with multiple priorities?' Some R&D roles ask about specific lab techniques or formulation experience.
