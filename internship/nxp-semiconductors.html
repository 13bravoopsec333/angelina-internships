📝 Description

High Performance Mixed Signal solutions. Automotive, industrial IoT, mobile, communication infrastructure.

✅ Eligibility

Currently enrolled students in engineering/technology programs

📋 Application Process

Applications open on nxp.com/careers (Workday ATS), typically in August–October for the following summer. Rolling review. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major match). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Technical + behavioral interview (45–60 min, virtual): mix of technical questions based on resume/projects and behavioral STAR. (5) Some roles may include a second technical interview. (6) Offer within 2–4 weeks. NXP is a major semiconductor company — major US offices in San Jose CA, Austin TX, and Chandler AZ. NXP focuses on automotive, industrial, IoT, and mobile semiconductor solutions.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on nxp.com/careers. Also available through Handshake and LinkedIn. NXP recruits at engineering schools with strong electrical engineering and materials science programs — UF is a target. Apply within the first 3 weeks. The San Jose CA location is the primary hub for engineering internships, with Austin TX as a secondary location.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA, Ansys Granta (materials selection — relevant to semiconductor materials), Creo/Solidworks (CAD tools), NMR/FTIR (materials characterization — transferable to semiconductor characterization), any semiconductor physics or materials science coursework. NXP values semiconductor materials knowledge. Keywords: semiconductor, silicon, thin films, materials characterization, process engineering, cleanroom, photonics, packaging. Frame your analytical skills (NMR, FTIR) as transferable to semiconductor materials analysis. If you have any Python/C++ programming, mention it.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 3 weeks. Your Materials Engineering background is relevant to semiconductor materials and process engineering. Prepare STAR stories about materials characterization, analytical problem-solving, and technical project work. NXP emphasizes innovation in automotive and industrial semiconductors — mention any interest in these application areas. If you have any cleanroom or thin film experience, highlight it. Return-offer rate estimated at 50–60%. NXP is a major semiconductor employer with ~34,000 employees globally.

No LeetCode-style coding for most engineering roles, but may include basic programming discussion.
