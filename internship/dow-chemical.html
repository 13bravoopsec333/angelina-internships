📝 Description

Internships in R&D, Manufacturing & Engineering, Supply Chain, and more. 1-5 year co-ops, local internships, and summer internships available globally. Mentorship and housing assistance for certain locations.

✅ Eligibility

Enrolled at accredited institution, GPA 3.0+

📋 Application Process

Apply via dow.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com. Online application with resume. Recruiter screen → hiring manager interview → site visit. Some roles have structured campus recruiting timelines (September-November for following summer). ADISE program for PhD students with data science skills.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply through Dow's Workday careers portal. Attend Dow campus recruiting events at UF. Network with Dow engineers at materials science conferences. Apply early—many positions fill in fall semester.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight chemical engineering and materials science coursework. Emphasize polymer research experience (Sumerlin group is directly relevant). Mention any process engineering or manufacturing experience. Include analytical skills (NMR, FTIR, JMP). Quantify research outcomes and metrics.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Dow is one of the largest chemical companies—emphasize broad technical skills. Your polymer research background is highly relevant. Show understanding of chemical processes and materials properties. Demonstrate ability to work in large corporate environments. Apply early in the recruiting cycle. Network through UF's materials science department connections.

🎤 Interview Format

Behavioral + technical interview. Expect STAR-method behavioral questions. Technical questions on chemistry, materials science, and process engineering. May include a presentation or case study. Site visit with team meetings.

🏆 Success Stories

Dow interns work on real projects that impact the business. Previous interns have worked on polymer development, process optimization, and materials characterization. Strong candidates demonstrate both technical depth and business awareness. Internship is a strong pipeline to full-time roles.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Application may ask: Describe your experience with polymer chemistry or materials development. How do you approach solving a complex technical problem? What interests you about working in the chemical industry?
