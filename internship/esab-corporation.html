📝 Description

Welding and cutting equipment. Engineering and safety internships.

✅ Eligibility

Students

📋 Application Process

Applications open on careers.esab.com (Workday ATS) or through ESAB's corporate website, typically in August–October for the following summer. ESAB was spun off from Colfax Corporation in 2022 and is a leading global provider of welding and cutting equipment, consumables, and automation solutions. Process: (1) Apply online with resume. (2) ATS screening (GPA 3.0+ floor, major, keywords). (3) Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min). (4) Hiring manager/technical interview (45–60 min, virtual): behavioral STAR + technical discussion. (5) Offer within 2–4 weeks. ESAB operates across multiple locations including headquarters in North Bethesda MD, and manufacturing facilities in Florence SC, Jenison MI, and other US locations.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply directly on careers.esab.com or through LinkedIn. Also available on Handshake and university career fairs. ESAB recruits at engineering schools with strong welding, materials, and manufacturing programs — UF's Materials Engineering program is relevant. Apply within the first 3 weeks. ESAB is a global company with ~9,000 employees — competition is moderate. Attend any ESAB info sessions for direct recruiter contact.

📄 Resume Tips

Highlight: Materials Engineering major, 3.96 GPA, Creo/Solidworks (CAD for welding equipment design), Ansys Granta (materials selection — directly relevant to filler metal and consumable selection), GD&T from HNI (precision manufacturing), PPAP (quality systems), NMR/FTIR (materials characterization — relevant to weld metallurgy analysis). Keywords: welding engineering, arc welding, consumables, metallurgy, manufacturing processes, quality systems, materials testing, automation. ESAB particularly values candidates with welding or metallurgy knowledge — mention any welding coursework or lab experience.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

Apply in the first 3 weeks. Target roles in welding engineering, product development, or manufacturing — your Materials Engineering background with metals/materials knowledge is ideal. ESAB values candidates who understand welding metallurgy and joining processes. Prepare STAR stories about manufacturing processes, quality systems, and materials characterization. ESAB emphasizes innovation in welding technology — mention any interest in welding science or joining technology. Return-offer rate estimated at 50–60%.

🎤 Interview Format

1–2 rounds. Recruiter phone screen (20–30 min) → Technical/behavioral interview with hiring manager (45–60 min). Glassdoor difficulty 2.5–3.0/5. Behavioral STAR questions plus technical discussion about welding processes, materials science, and manufacturing. Some positions may include a brief technical assessment or welding knowledge quiz.

🏆 Success Stories

Glassdoor employee rating ~3.4/5. ESAB is the world's largest standalone welding and cutting company with ~9,000 employees globally. The company was recently spun off from Colfax and is investing heavily in growth and talent acquisition. Interns report exposure to cutting-edge welding technology, hands-on experience with welding processes, and meaningful project work. ESAB has been recognized as a top employer in the manufacturing sector.

📝 Supplemental Questions

No supplemental essays required. Online application includes standard screening questions (GPA, work authorization, location preference, citizenship status for some positions). Some roles may ask about willingness to work in a manufacturing environment or travel to plant locations.
