📝 Description

10-16 week research internships under DOE WDTS program. Mentored by leading scientists across materials science, chemistry, and engineering.

✅ Eligibility

Full-time undergrad in STEM, 3.0+ GPA, US citizen/permanent resident

📋 Application Process

Apply through internships.energy.gov (DOE centralized portal). Requires: transcript, resume, statement of interest/research interests, faculty recommendation letter. Two application cycles per year (spring/fall deadlines). PNNL also recruits directly via Handshake for their own university internship positions. The SULI program specifically runs through DOE's Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internship portal. After submission, applications are reviewed by lab mentors who select based on research fit. Phone/video interviews typically conducted by the mentor PI.

🚀 Best Way to Apply

Apply via internships.energy.gov for SULI. Also check PNNL's direct careers portal (careers.pnnl.gov) and Handshake for non-SULI internship postings. PNNL actively recruits on Handshake and has won 6 consecutive Early Talent Awards from the platform. Email PNNL mentors directly in your research area of interest for the best chance.

📄 Resume Tips

Lead with: Materials Science/Engineering coursework, polymer research experience (Sumerlin group), NMR and TLC analytical techniques, chain polymerization knowledge, Ansys Granta materials selection, Creo/Solidworks CAD, GD&T from HNI internship, FTIR spectroscopy. Highlight GPA 3.96 prominently. Include specific research outcomes from Sumerlin group (any publications, presentations). PNNL materials research areas: catalysis, nuclear materials, battery materials, additive manufacturing, materials characterization — tailor statement of interest to match a specific PNNL research group.

🎯 Maximize Your Chances

1) Email PNNL mentors directly before applying — find their names on PNNL's research pages for materials science groups (Computational Materials Science, Materials Synthesis & Characterization). 2) Apply to BOTH SULI (DOE portal) AND PNNL's direct Handshake postings. 3) In your statement of interest, reference specific PNNL papers or projects. 4) UF Materials Science faculty may have PNNL collaborators — ask your advisor for introductions. 5) Apply early in the application window. 6) Follow up with a thank-you email after any interview. Timeline: SULI applications typically open ~6 months before start date.

🎤 Interview Format

Phone or video interview (15-30 min) with the potential mentor/PI. Questions focus on: research interests, specific techniques you want to learn, how your background prepares you, career goals. May include technical questions about your prior research. Some mentors ask about specific publications they've written. The mentor has significant say in selection — building rapport matters more than formal interview performance.

🏆 Success Stories

Reddit users on r/EngineeringStudents and r/ChemicalEngineering have reported successful SULI applications by tailoring their research statements to specific mentor projects. Success factors mentioned: having prior research experience (especially lab techniques like NMR), strong faculty recommendation, and genuine interest in the specific lab's work. Students with 3.5+ GPA and prior research publications reported high success rates. PNNL specifically values hands-on instrumentation skills.

📝 Supplemental Questions

Statement of interest explaining: your research interests, why you want to work at PNNL specifically, what skills you bring, how the experience fits your career goals. Faculty recommendation letter is required. unofficial transcript.
